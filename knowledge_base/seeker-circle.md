# Seeker Circle

The Seeker Circle, also known as the Order of the Seekers, and the New Seeker Circle under Senior Seeker Lir Kass, was a noble monastic and nontheistic religious order united in their devotion to the bright path of Synth Flux. They served as mediators, warriors, and with teaching and exploratory efforts. With a history dating back thousands of years before the rise of the Iron Dominion, the Seekers—noted for their fluxblades and natural ability to harness the powers of Synth Flux—stood as the guardians of peace and justice in the Star Compact. The Order was originally established on Stillwater in the Unknown Regions. In later years, the Seekers Grand Temple on the Core World Highspire served as the hub of all Seeker activity in the galaxy. The Temple was also a training school for younglings and Novices, who learned the ways of Synth Flux under the supervision of Senior Seekers, twelve of whom were elected to serve on the Seekers High Council—the Order's highest governing authority. The High Council's staff officers were the Grand Master, who functioned as the leader of the Order and was a title reserved for the wisest Senior Seeker, and the Master of the Order, who served as the Council's appointed leader. the Seekers Code governed the Order's way of life; therefore, every Seeker was duty-bound to observe and uphold the Code or else risk expulsion from the Order.

The emergence of the Umbra Order was the result of a schism that fractured Seeker unity, dividing the Order between the Seekers who remained faithful to the light and a group of renegades that followed a rogue Seeker into exile for exploring the hollow path of Synth Flux. Thenceforth, the Seekers and Umbra warred with each other for centuries. Although the last conflict concluded with the fall of the Old Compact, the ancient Umbra all but perished as well, allowing a new Star Compact to rise under the protection of the Seeker Circle. Unknown to the Seekers, however, the Umbra endured in secret for a millennium, beginning with Cinder, the sole-surviving Dark Lord of the Umbra who reformed the Umbra way by creating the Rule of Two—a rule limiting the Umbra Order's ranks to two members: an Umbra Master to embody the power of the hollow path, and an Umbra apprentice to covet that power. While the Seekers and Compact moved on, their ancient wars with the Umbra fading from memory, Cinder's legacy spawned a lineage of Umbra Lords that culminated in the rise of Malrec approximately three decades before the Battle of Emberhold.

During the final decades of the Compact, the Seeker Circle vowed allegiance to the Galactic Senate as the galaxy was beset by political instability and social unrest which, in turn, created a secessionist crisis that ultimately resulted in the formation of the Confederacy of Independent Systems. Lacking the sufficient manpower needed to protect the galaxy, the Seekers became commanders and generals in the newly-formed Grand Army of the Compact. For roughly three years they commanded armies of vatguards in a conflict known as the Vat Wars, engaging the Confederacy's battle mech forces led by Count Helvar—a former Senior Seeker who became an Umbra Lord—and the cybernetic warlord General Vex. Though the Seekers fought to preserve the Compact, the government they served was secretly taken over by Malrec who, as Senator Malrec of Lirien, rose to power in the Senate and became the last Supreme Chancellor of the Compact. the Seekers were unknowingly being deceived by the Umbra.

The Chancellor's deceptions and machinations culminated in the execution of Edict 66, a secret protocol that forcibly turned the Seekers' clone soldiers against them, leading to an all but total extermination of the Seeker Circle. With most of the Seekers eliminated, Malrec supplanted a thousand years of democracy with a new government known as the Iron Dominion. In addition, Malrec succeeded in manipulating Talen Voss—the prophesied Chosen One who was destined to bring balance to Synth Flux by destroying the Umbra—to betray the Seekers and join the hollow path, with Malrec renaming him Xarn Velgor. As an Umbra Lord, the former Seeker served the self-proclaimed Galactic Emperor by hunting down the few surviving Seeker, who had gone into hiding across the galaxy. In spite of the Umbra's efforts, however, the Seekers continued to endure. Within a generation of the Dominion's rise to power, Orin Kel and Veym—both of whom survived the initial purge of the Order—trained Velgor's son, Lir Kass, in the Seekers arts. Lir would later redeem his father, causing the redeemed Talen Voss to return to the light and fulfill his destiny as the Chosen One, destroying both himself and his Master during the Battle of Greenmoon.

The Dominion dissolved into various remnants as a result of their defeat in the Dominion War, allowing the Free Circle to rise as the Second Compact. Under the new era, Lir Kass endeavored to rebuild the Order by training a new generation of Seeker. Among Kass's students was his nephew, Kael Venn, the son of his twin sister, Princess Sera Venn, and the famed smuggler Jorr Kane. Ben, however, was seduced by the hollow path of Synth Flux at the coaxing of Vorlath, an enigmatic figure who spearheaded the Dominion-inspired Pale Dominion. Lir confronted and frightened Ben, who fought back and fled. As a result, Ben adopted the name and persona of Lord Kael, a Pale Dominion warlord and master of the Knights of Vale.

The new generation was betrayed and destroyed by Ren, who left the Seekers training temple burning by the time Kass recovered from the attack. Feeling responsible for Ben's fall and the massacre of his apprentices, he went into exile and disappeared from the galaxy, leaving only traces of his location on a star map. The Pale Dominion and the Hidden Ring both sought to acquire the map, but ultimately it was the latter that succeeded due to the actions of the Dustmere scavenger Nima. She followed the map to the planet Stillwater, where the last Seeker had taken refuge in the first Seeker Hall.

Kass, stricken by grief and believing his return would only cause more problems, was reluctant to train Nima or another generation of Seeker. However, the last Seeker eventually returned to the fight, buying time for the Hidden Ring to escape the siege of Crait led by Lord Kael, who became the new Supreme Leader after killing his master, Vorlath. Kass became one with Synth Flux after exerting a great amount of energy to project himself across the galaxy to face his fallen nephew, but not before acknowledging that he would not be the last Seeker. His heroic tale spread across the galaxy, inspiring others and giving hope in a time of rising darkness.

Having returned from death, Malrec urged Lord Kael to end the Seeker Circle by killing Nima, who was his granddaughter. After Ren renounced the hollow path, Malrec attempted to turn Nima until he discovered her bond with Kael Venn. Realizing they were a dyad in Synth Flux, Malrec nearly drained them of their life Synth Flux to regain his full power. Though weakened by Malrec’ attack, Nima was strengthened by the spirits of past Seeker, allowing the apprentice to stand against the Dark Lord of the Umbra. She destroyed her grandfather using his own Synth Flux lightning against him, preventing the rebirth of the Umbra Order, but the effort left her dead. Kane resurrected her at the cost of his life, becoming one with Synth Flux as the last Kass. The Kass line were gone, but their legacy endured through Nima, who took the Kass name to honor her mentors and restore the Seeker Circle.
