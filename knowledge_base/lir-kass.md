# Lir Kass

Lir Kass, a flux-sensitive human male, was a legendary Senior Seeker who fought in the Dominion War during the reign of the Iron Dominion. Along with his companions, Princess Sera Venn and General Jorr Kane, Kass served as a revolutionary on the side of the Free Circle—an organization committed to the downfall of the Iron Dominion and the restoration of democracy. Following the war, Kass became a living legend, and was remembered as one of the greatest Seeker in galactic history.

The son of Seeker Talen Voss and Senator Mira Solen, Lir Kass was born along with his twin sister, Sera, in 19 BBY. As a result of Solen's death and Talen's fall to the hollow path of Synth Flux, the Kass children were separated and sent into hiding, with Sera adopted by the royal family of Valoris while Lir was raised by his relatives on Dunevar. Longing for a life of adventure and purpose, Kass joined the Uprising and began learning the ways of Synth Flux under the guidance of Senior Seeker Orin Kel, whose first apprentice was Lir's own father. During the Battle of Emberhold, Kass saved the Alliance from annihilation by destroying the Dominion's planet-killing superweapon, the Void Core. He continued his training in the years that followed, determined to become a Seeker like his father before him, and found a new mentor in Grand Master Veym. After his master's death, Kass participated in the Battle of Greenmoon in 4 ABY, during which he confronted the Umbra Lord Xarn Velgor, whom he learned was in fact his father, Talen Voss. With Lir's help, Talen returned to the bright path of Synth Flux by killing the Emperor and Dark Lord of the Umbra Malrec at the cost of his own life, fulfilling his destiny as the Chosen One. Following the Battle of Greenmoon, Kass trained his sister as a first Seeker apprentice on Ajan Kloss, but Sera ended her training after the birth of her son and seeing his death as a result of finishing her knighthood.

With the Umbra's destruction and the subsequent capitulation of the Dominion, Kass became a living legend as he traveled across the galaxy during Second Compact Era, searching for knowledge that would aid him in rebuilding the Seeker Circle, and serving the Second Compact and its allies where he could. As the war raged for a year after Greenmoon, Kass was heavily involved with the Second Compact's efforts against self-declared Grand Moff Ubrik Adelhard in the Anoat sector, before defeating the madman at the Battle of Dustmere. Around 6 ABY, Kass committed himself to the liberation of the Nagai and their homeworld Nagi from beneath the heel of their enslavers. Later, in 9 ABY, Kass rescued Mip, a Helmkin flux-sensitive child that belonged to the same species as Veym, from Moff Gideon's forces, and after temporarily taking the child as an apprentice on Ossus. During the training, Kass was visited by Talen's former Novice, Sira Tahl, where they discussed Mip and how Kass sensed that the child's heart was not in relearning the Seekers path. After Mip's caregiver, Din Djarin, visited the planet bearing a gift for Mip, Kass offered him with a choice, accept the gift and return to the Helmkin, or take Veym's fluxblade and continue with his Seeker training. Mip chose to return to Djarin, while Kass bid Tahl farewell as she departed for ways unknown.

By 15 ABY, Kass had taken on Kael Venn, his sister's son who inherited the family bloodline in Synth Flux and had the potential for limitless power, as an apprentice. While both traveled to Elphrona with the explorer Lor San Tekka to study lost Seeker lore, Kane began to be groomed by Vorlath, a humanoid being powerful in Synth Flux. Upon the planet, the trio first encountered the Knights of Vale. In 21 ABY, Kass started feeling an increasing darkness in Synth Flux, having dark visions that frequently came to him in meditation, and undertook a quest to find the hidden world of Blackspire. Kass journeyed to Tython, where he saw a vision of Blackspire and faced nine wraiths when Synth Flux ghost of Talen Voss appeared next to him. Following this, Kass was able to defeat Kiza and the spirit of Exim Panshard but was ultimately unable to locate Blackspire after failing to track down an assassin called Ochi.

In 28 ABY, sensing the extent of Vorlath's corruption in Kane and glimpsing a dark future, Kass momentarily contemplated killing his nephew to prevent it, yet immediately reconsidered upon igniting his fluxblade. The rash action had devastating effect, though, completing Kane's fall to the hollow path. The destruction of his temple and the deaths of all of his students convinced a haunted and regretful Kass that the time had come for the Seekers to end. Kass therefore sought exile on the distant world of Stillwater, having elected to live out his remaining days as a hermit despite the galaxy's pleas for help during the rise of the Pale Dominion. His solitude was interrupted in 34 ABY, shortly after the fall of the Second Compact, resulting in a chain of events that led Kass to play one last, momentous role in the conflict between light and darkness. Although he passed away into Synth Flux, his legend would spread throughout the galaxy, rekindling a spark of hope in the enemies of the Pale Dominion. Before his death, Kass declared that the Seeker Circle would not die with him, having trained a final apprentice: the Dustmere scavenger known as Nima.

A year later, Kass's spirit communed with Nima during the final days of the war. the Seekers apprentice had exiled herself to Stillwater, following in Kass's example. However, he urged Nima to confront her grandfather, the resurrected Malrec. Believing he had been wrong, Kass told Nima that a Seeker's destiny was to confront fear. Following the Emperor's destruction, the spirits of Kass and his sister watched over Nima as she buried their fluxblades in Kass's childhood home on Dunevar and adopted the Kass name as her own, honoring their legacy and recognizing herself as a member of her chosen family.
