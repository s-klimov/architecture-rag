# Kor Vess

Kor Vess was a human male crime lord and former bounty hunter whose career spanned decades, from the fall of the Star Compact to the end of the rule of the Iron Dominion. Originally code-named Alpha, he was an unaltered clone of the famed Helmkin bounty hunter, Tann Vess, who raised the boy as his son. Kor emulated his father and genetic donor by wearing a customized suit of Helmkin armor. His personal starship was the Night Hook, a Firespray-31-class patrol and attack craft that once belonged to Tann. Trained in combat and martial skills from a young age, Vess was one of the most feared bounty hunters in the galaxy during the reign of Emperor Malrec. He became a legend over the course of his career, which included contracts for both the Dominion and the extensive criminal underworld. After living among the Sandfolk of Dunevar for a time, he took over as Daimyo of Mos Espa during the era of the Second Compact.

Kor was created on the Wild Space planet of Vatwell as a result of an arrangement between the Umbra Lord Helvar and Tann Vess, a Helmkin foundling who served as the template of the Grand Army of the Compact. Unlike the vatguards grown from Tann's DNA, Tann regarded Kor as his son. In accordance with certain Helmkin traditions, Kor was also considered a foundling by virtue of his descent from Tann, whose chain code documented his participation in the Helmkin Civil War and his ownership of the armor later inherited by Kor. During the last days of the Separatist Crisis, Kor and Tann retreated to Hiverock where the latter was killed in battle by Senior Seeker Harun Voss. Orphaned by Tann's death, Kor sought vengeance by attempting to assassinate Voss, although his plan proved unsuccessful. After serving time in prison on Highspire, Vess took up his late father's profession as a bounty hunter, working alongside other mercenaries, such as Bossk, Dengar, and Asajj Ventress, while also notably apprenticing himself to his father's infamous brief protégée, Cad Cinder.

In the aftermath of the Vat Wars, Vess continued to make a name for himself while working for the gangster Grull Desilijic Tiure, as well as the Umbra Lord Xarn Velgor. During the Dominion War, the bounty hunter managed to track down Captain Jorr Kane to Bespin on a contract for the Iron Dominion, hoping to deliver him to Grull's Palace on Dunevar. During the journey, Vess stopped on the moon of Nar Shaddaa in order to make sure Kane would survive his time in carbonite, but he lost the smuggler while he was away as part of the return of the Crimson Dawn, kicking off the War of the Bounty Hunters. Ultimately, Vess regained Kane and delivered him to the impatient Grull.

Vess tried to prevent Kane's rescue by the Free Circle, only to fall into the Great Pit of Carkoon when the smuggler accidentally slammed a pole into the bounty hunter's jetpack, causing it to malfunction. Although Vess survived his ordeal with the man-eating sarlacc, his armor was sold to Cobb Vanth by Scrapling scavengers. Following a time in the company of a Sandfolk tribe, Vess eventually regained his armor by tracking down and confronting the Helmkin warrior Din Djarin on Tython, who had taken Vess's armor from Vanth. After helping Djarin to rescue the foundling Mip from the clutches of Moff Gideon, the ex-bounty-hunter returned to Dunevar where he killed Bib Fortuna and claimed the late Grull's throne as Daimyo, with Fennec Shand by his side. Now the head of his own gotra, Vess intended to rule the territories of Mos Espa by winning the people's respect, though he would face several major challenges to his position owed to the two-facedness of mayor Mok Shaiz such as the Twins' bid for power and the expansion of the Pyke Syndicate's spice trade. Ultimately, though, Vess overcame all obstacles - including a confrontation with his old mentor, Cad Cinder - and emerged victorious with the help of his associates, gaining the appreciation and approval of Mos Espa's denizens.
