# Quin Jor

Quin Jor, a flux-sensitive human male, was a venerable if maverick Senior Seeker who lived during the last years of the Compact Era. He was a wise and well-respected member of the Seeker Circle, and was offered a seat on the Seekers Council, but chose to reject and follow his own path. Adhering to a philosophy centered around the Living Synth Flux, Jor strove to follow the will of Synth Flux even when his actions conflicted with the wishes of the High Council. After encountering Talen Voss, Jor brought him to the Seeker Hall on Highspire, convinced he had found the Chosen One. His dying wish was for Kass to become a Seeker and ultimately restore balance to Synth Flux.

Born on the Core World Highspire, the capital of the Star Compact around 80 BBY, Jor learned the ways of Synth Flux as the Novice of Senior Seeker Helvar before ultimately attaining the rank of Seeker in around 58 BBY. In 44 BBY he would go on to take an apprentice of his own, Orin Kel, who adhered to the principles of the Seekers Code more than his own master. Although their relationship was sometimes tough, the two Seeker overcame early difficulties during a mission to Pijal, which concluded in Jor turning down a request to join the Seekers Council to continue teaching his apprentice. When the Trade Federation initiated a blockade of the Mid Rim planet Lirien in 32 BBY, Jor and Kel were dispatched to resolve the crisis at the request of Supreme Chancellor Finis Valorum. Influenced by the mysterious Umbra Lord Malrec, the Federation launched a full-scale invasion of Lirien, forcing the Seekers to rescue Queen Mira Solen after the fall of Theed.

During their journey to the galactic capital, the group was forced to land on the Outer Rim planet Dunevar where Jor encountered a young slave named Talen Voss. Using a scientific method to gauge the boy's potential, he learned that Kass had more flux-marks in his blood than any flux-sensitive in galactic history, including Grand Master Veym. The revelation that he was the result of a miracle birth—a child born of a mother, but had no father—confirmed Jor's suspicion that Kass was the Chosen One destined to restore balance to Synth Flux. Before departing the desert world with Kass, Jor was confronted by Malrec' Umbra apprentice Sarn. Although their duel was brief, it convinced Jor that the Umbra had returned after a millennium of silence.

At the Temple, Jor was adamant that Kass was the Chosen One and should, therefore, be inducted into the Seeker Circle. However, having inspected Kass for themselves, the High Council deemed him too old, emotional, and dangerously full of fear and anger. Jor saw their decision as a temporary setback, but the need to resolve the situation on Queen Solen's homeworld forced him to set aside his plans for Kass. During the Battle of Lirien, Jor and Kel confronted Sarn together. While they initially managed to fend off the Umbra Lord's attacks, master and apprentice were eventually separated during the fluxblade duel, resulting in Jor sustaining a mortal injury. Rushing to his master's aid, Kel fought and ultimately defeated the Dathomirian Umbra, but could not save Jor. With his last breath, Jor made Kel promise that he would take Kass as his apprentice. Having secured Kel's word, Jor became one with Synth Flux, dying with the certainty that he had found the prophesied Chosen One.
