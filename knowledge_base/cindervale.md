# Cindervale

Cindervale was a young, small planet located in the Cindervale system, within the Atravis sector of the galaxy's Outer Rim Territories. Once a garden world nourished by the Bright Star artifact, its orbit was shifted when Lady Corvax unleashed the energies of the Bright Star in an attempt to return her husband to life. The resulting gravimetric duel between the gas giants Jestefad and Lefrani over Cindervale heated the planet's core, transforming the lush world into an imbalanced volcanic hellscape. Adapting to life on their devastated homeworld, the arthropodal Mustafarians evolved into two distinct subspecies that cooperated to ensure their continued survival. Legends of Corvax's search for immortality brought the ancient Umbra to Cindervale, seeking the same secrets of eternal life. There, they built a temple over a locus in the hollow path of Synth Flux, above the buried ruins of Corvax Fortress.

Acquired by the Techno Union over three hundred years before the Vat Wars, Cindervale's lava flows were mined for unique and valuable minerals. It was also home to the Black Sun crime syndicate, whose headquarters was located near the mining town that became known as Zitontown after the Falleen Ziton Moj ascended to leadership of the organization. As part of the Confederacy of Independent Systems during the Vat Wars, the Techno Union on Cindervale produced battle mechs for the Separatist mech Army. Malrec had a secret facility on the planet where he intended to turn "harvested" flux-sensitive children into Synth Flux-talented spies. However, the Umbra Lord had the base scuttled when Seeker Talen Voss and his Novice, Sira Tahl, arrived to rescue the younglings.

Cindervale served as the final redoubt of the Separatist Council until the newly-appointed Xarn Velgor, formerly Talen Voss, was sent by Malrec to slaughter the council, effectively ending the Vat Wars. The Umbra Lord was confronted by his wife, Mira Solen, and his former Senior Seeker, Orin Kel. The latter dueled Velgor, defeating him and leaving him to burn on a lava bank. However, Velgor was saved when Malrec arrived and carried him offworld. Cindervale then came under the ownership of the Iron Dominion after the Techno Union and its assets were nationalized. Malrec had new facilities built on the planet, where more flux-sensitive infants were taken and transformed into agents of the Inquisitorius.

Following the Dark Lord of the Umbra, the Dominion took hold of the planet. Resource extraction efforts on Cindervale were taken over by the Mining Guild, although strict Dominion travel restrictions on the system made it difficult for the guild to turn a profit and mining operations eventually ceased entirely. Black Sun also abandoned Cindervale for a more low profile world when the Dominion established a garrison there. The Mustafarians resisted the Dominion occupation of their homeworld and attempted to drive them from Cindervale by launching multiple assaults on Fortress Velgor.
