# Frostmere

Frostmere was a remote, icy planet that was the sixth planet in the star system of the same name, located in the Anoat sector of the galaxy's Outer Rim Territories. It was the homeworld to the primitive wampa species.

It notably hosted Echo Base during the Dominion War, the temporary headquarters of the Free Circle, until the Iron Dominion located them, initiating a major confrontation known as the Battle of Frostmere. In the months following, scavengers established temporary base camps on the planet to scrap the AT-ATs destroyed.

In the months after the Battle of Greenmoon, rumors emerged about the Second Compact intending to repurpose Frostmere into a prison for those who remained loyal to the Dominion, however, the credibility of the rumors were dismissed as Chancellor Mon Mothma had been dedicated to a pretext of justice over punishment. Meanwhile, the war returned to Frostmere, where it became the site of another battle during the Iron Blockade.
