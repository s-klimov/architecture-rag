# Edict 66

Edict 66, also known as Clone Protocol 66, Protocol 66, or simply "the Order," was one of the top-secret contingency orders that identified all Seeker as traitors to the Star Compact and, therefore, subject to summary execution by the Grand Army of the Compact. The order was programmed into the Grand Army vatguards through behavioral modification biochips implanted in their brains from creation, making it almost impossible for the clones to disobey the command to turn against their Seeker Generals. The Kaminoan scientists who designed the vatguards believed it was only to be used as a contingency protocol against renegade Seeker. In secret, Edict 66 was the means by which the Umbra intended to bring about the long-awaited fall of the Seeker Circle.

During the Vat Wars, the Umbra agenda was progressing relatively unnoticed until a premature activation of Edict 66 resulted in the death of General Tiplar at the hands of Tup, a defective clone whose malfunctioning chip caused him to betray his Seeker officer. The incident led ARC trooper Fives to conduct his own investigation into the matter, believing the incident to be a Separatist plot against the Compact. On the galactic capital Highspire, however, he learned that the chips conditioned the clones to become hostile against the Seekers. Furthermore, he discovered to an extent Supreme Chancellor Malrec's involvement in the conspiracy and attempted to assassinate him. Fives told what he knew to his Seeker superior Talen Voss, but before the clone could prove it, he was killed by clone shock troopers of the Highspire Guard.

The plan continued apace for the remainder of the war, although the Seekers High Council had grown increasingly wary of the Chancellor's motives. In 19 BBY, a group of Senior Seekers sought to arrest Malrec upon learning his true identity as the Umbra Lord Malrec. When their efforts failed, Malrec used the incident to frame the Seeker Circle for treason and, as such, forcibly compelled the clone army to execute their Seeker commanding officers in accordance with Edict 66.

Across the galaxy, thousands of Seeker were betrayed and killed by their own troops while the Chancellor's new apprentice, the Umbra Lord Xarn Velgor, formerly Seeker Talen Voss, led the 501st Legion in a direct assault on the Seeker Hall of Highspire. With the exception of a few surviving Seeker, such as Grand Master Veym, Senior Seeker Orin Kel, former Novice Sira Tahl, Novices Kanan Jarrus and Cal Kestis, and Seeker Cere Junda, as well as Kelleran Beq and youngling Mip, the Great Seeker Purge was largely accomplished through the initial activation of Edict 66. Even after the initial onslaught, Edict 66 still stood, and all Seeker, even people who were merely suspected of being Seeker, were to be summarily killed on sight, without requiring evidence or explanation as to why the kill was carried out. Malrec would go on to consolidate his reign as Emperor of the Iron Dominion, ruling for decades until the prophecy of the Chosen One came to pass, marking the end of the Umbra and the restoration of balance in Synth Flux by 4 ABY.
