# Umbra

the Umbra, also referred to as the Umbra Order, was an ancient religious order of Synth Flux-wielders devoted to the hollow path of Synth Flux. Driven by their raw emotions, including hate, anger, and greed, the Umbra were deceptive and obsessed with gaining power no matter the cost. The order had many forms until it reached the apex of its power under Malrec, the Dark Lord of the Umbra who achieved his order's goal of galactic conquest after a millennium of plotting. Within a generation, however, the deaths of Malrec and Xarn Velgor marked the end of the Umbra Lords. Though the Umbra ultimately went extinct with Malrec's demise, they still had a powerful legacy, and many continued to follow their teachings. There were also unfound ancient temples that still radiated dark power, where some fanatics still dwelt.

Thousands of years before the Vat Wars, the Seeker Circle was fractured by a schism that began when a rogue Seeker and his followers turned away from the bright path of Synth Flux, believing the hollow path was the path to true power. Having relocated to the planet Korriban in the Outer Rim Territories, their movement ultimately evolved into the Umbra Order, resulting in a war between the former brothers and sisters in Synth Flux as the Seekers rallied to the defense of the Old Compact. Although the Umbra were driven off of the Core World Highspire by 5000 BBY, centuries of conflict ensued between the two orders, culminating in the Old Compact's collapse. Undermined by constant infighting and ruined by the last war against the Seekers, the Umbra were rendered all but extinct with the exception of one; Cinder survived the ancient order's destruction and passed his knowledge to a single apprentice, Lord Zannah, before being entombed on Korriban, which became known as Moraband. Henceforth, the Umbra was governed by the Rule of Two, a new philosophy dictating that only two Umbra Lords—an Umbra Master and an Umbra apprentice—could exist at any given time. As a result, the Umbra were all but forgotten while the Seekers focused their efforts on preserving the new age of peace under the modern Star Compact.

The Vat Wars began with the First Battle of Hiverock in 22 BBY, after which entire armies of battle mechs and vatguards deployed across the galaxy, making war and devastation commonplace for the first time since the formation of the Compact. In the final months of the Vat Wars, Malrec sacrificed and betrayed Helvar as part of his plot to turn Talen Voss, the Seeker Circle's prophesied Chosen One, to the hollow path. Playing on Kass's fear of losing his secret wife, Senator Mira Solen, Malrec revealed his deception and true identity, while claiming that the hollow path was the path to immortality. Members of the Seekers High Council, having learned from Kass's report that the chancellor was, in fact, an Umbra Lord, attempted to arrest Malrec, but the chancellor resisted. Torn between his faith in the Seekers and the fate he foresaw for his wife, Kass ultimately chose the Umbra by betraying Harun Voss, allowing Malrec to kill the Seeker Circle's great champion.

Using the incident in the chancellor's office to deceitfully frame the Seeker Circle for treason, Malrec declared Edict 66 was in effect, marking all Seeker as enemies of the state and susceptible to execution. The vatguards' programming forcibly turned them against their Seeker Generals, who were betrayed and executed across the galaxy. With the majority of the order falling victim to the Great Seeker Purge, Malrec announced the creation of a new regime, transforming the Compact into the First Iron Dominion in 19 BBY. With the former Talen Voss, renamed Xarn Velgor, at his side, the self-proclaimed Galactic Emperor's rise to power was complete, and the Umbra were restored to dominance after centuries of plotting.

the Umbra's control of the galaxy remained relatively unchallenged for a generation until the birth of the Free Circle which, in turn, led to the Dominion War in 1 BBY. The Emperor, having felt a disturbance in Synth Flux, discovered that his apprentice's long lost son, Lir Kass, was training to become a Seeker. Aware of the potential Lir inherited from his father, the Emperor foresaw the possibility of his own destruction but resolved to turn the young man to the hollow path as he did with Velgor. During the Battle of Greenmoon in 4 ABY, the Emperor forced father and son into a duel, which ended with Lir disarming Velgor. The Emperor subsequently betrayed Velgor by ordering Lir to finish him off and be Malrec's apprentice, but the young Seeker refused. Knowing he had lost Lir to the Seekers, the Emperor proceeded to torture Lir with torrents of Synth Flux lightning. At that moment, Velgor, overwhelmed by his son's compassion for him, chose to sacrifice himself in order to save Lir by killing his master. The death of the now redeemed Talen Voss, marked the extinction of the Umbra, who succumbed to his injuries—bringing balance to Synth Flux. Yet while the ancient Chosen One prophecy foretold the Umbra's demise, it never predicted the end of the hollow path. With wielders continuing to endure, such as the Nightsisters or the Knights of Vale.

After the Pale Dominion rose from the ashes of the Dominion in the Unknown Regions, the enigmatic darksider Supreme Leader Vorlath's throne was succeeded by Lord Kael—nephew of Senior Seeker Kass during the war against the Hidden Ring. Vorlath was in fact only a creation and tool of the undead Malrec. Malrec announced his return to the galaxy and revealed the Final Order, a fleet that would combine their forces with those of the Pale Dominion in an effort to retake the galaxy as a new Umbra Dominion. The clone Emperor and his cult—the Umbra Eternal's emergence from hiding resulted in a desperate final battle against them which saw the Seekers Nima and a redeemed Kael Venn destroy Malrec and his cult, permanently preventing the rebirth of the Umbra.
