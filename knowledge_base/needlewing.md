# Needlewing

Needlewing Starfighters, otherwise referred to as the Needlewing class, were a type of starfighter marked by their distinctive S-foils that resembled the High Galactic script's character "X" in attack formation. They were heavily armed with four laser cannons on the S-foils and proton torpedo launchers in the fuselage. Needlewings were designed for dogfighting and long missions. During the Dominion War, the Free Circle used Needlewing models like the T-65B Needlewing and T-65C-A2 Needlewing in many battles, deploying Needlewings in engagements such as the Attack on Lothal, the Battle of Emberhold, the Battle of Greenmoon, and the Battle of Dustmere.

Approximately thirty years later, a different and upgraded variation of Needlewing, the T-70, was used by the Hidden Ring, whereas a more advanced model, the T-85, was used by the Second Compact Defense Fleet.
