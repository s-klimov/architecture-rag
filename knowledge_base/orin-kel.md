# Orin Kel

Orin Kel was a legendary flux-sensitive human male Senior Seeker who served on the Seekers High Council during the final years of the Compact Era. As a High Seeker General, Kel served in the Grand Army of the Compact that fought against the Separatist mech Army during the Vat Wars, and was nicknamed "the Negotiator." Kel, however, was forced into exile as a result of the Great Seeker Purge initiated by the Compact's own vatguards when Compact Supreme Chancellor, secretly Malrec, implemented Edict 66. As a mentor, Kel was responsible for training two members of the Kass family, Talen and Lir Kass, both of whom served in turn as his Novice in the ways of Synth Flux.

Kel was born on the planet Stewjon in 57 BBY. He was flux-sensitive, and therefore taken from his family and inducted into the Seeker Circle as a child. Raised in the Seekers Grand Temple on the planet Highspire, Kel was assigned to Senior Seeker Quin Jor. In 32 BBY, Kel attained the rank of Seeker by defeating the Umbra Lord Sarn during the Invasion of Lirien. He also chose to train Talen Voss—the prophesied Chosen One who was destined to destroy the Umbra and restore balance to Synth Flux—having promised Jor who died in the fluxblade duel against Sarn.

With the advent of the Vat Wars in 22 BBY, Kel adopted the military rank of High Seeker General in the Grand Army of the Star Compact, and was given command of the Open Circle Fleet, the Third Systems Army, and the 7th Sky Corps. Clone Marshal Commander Cody served as his second-in-command throughout the galaxy-wide conflict, with their forces achieving multiple victories over the Confederacy of Independent Systems. In the midst of the war, his old enemy Sarn re-emerged into galactic affairs, bent on revenge against Kel for his debilitating defeat decades prior. Though they clashed in heated duels on multiple occasions, Sarn failed to end Kel's life or cripple his spirit, despite murdering his former love, Satine Kryze, Duchess of Mandalore.

Ten years later, Kel left exile to rescue Sera Venn, Lir's twin sister, and was pursued by the Dominion's Inquisitorius and Velgor himself. Later, Kel was hunted down once again by Sarn, whom he finally slew to protect the young Lir. Kel began the boy's Seeker training in 1 BBY, and soon after encountered Velgor aboard the first Void Core, where he sacrificed himself to ensure that Lir and his allies escaped from the Umbra Lord. In death, Kel became one with Synth Flux which allowed him to continue guiding Lir throughout the Dominion War. In 4 ABY, Kel reunited with his former student, Talen Voss, who died destroying his Umbra Master in order to save his son. Kel was remembered as a legendary Senior Seeker during the rise of the Second Compact; Kael Venn, the son of Lir's twin sister, Princess Sera Venn, was named after Kel. Kane's turn to the hollow path left Lir disillusioned with the Seeker Circle's legacy, and he held Kel responsible for Talen's transformation into Velgor. However, Lir's faith would eventually be restored with the aid of Malrec's granddaughter Nima, to whom Kel would lend his voice and strength in a final battle and victory against her resurrected ancestor on Blackspire in 35 ABY.
