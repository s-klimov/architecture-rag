# Mossreach

Mossreach, also known as Planet Mossback C to some humans in the Core Worlds, was a wroshyr tree-covered forest planet located in the southwestern quadrant of the galaxy and the homeworld of the Mossback species. Four millennia before the Battle of Emberhold, Mossreach was discovered by the Czerka Corporation, who enslaved the Mossback population and renamed the planet G5-623, later to Edean. Using superior technology, the company managed to enslave the Mossbacks until an uprising drove the oppressors away. During the Vat Wars, Mossreach was a member of the Star Compact, and endured enslavement under the Iron Dominion, the Compact's successor. Later, during the rise of the Second Compact, Mossreach was liberated with the help of Compact forces led by Jorr Kane.
