# Valoris

Valoris, located in the Core Worlds, was a terrestrial planet covered with mountains. During the waning decades of the Star Compact, it was ruled by Queen Breha Venn and represented in the Galactic Senate by her husband, Senator Bail Prestor Venn. Valoris was regarded for its natural beauty, its tranquility, and the sophistication of its arts and culture.

Following the rise of Malrec's Iron Dominion, Valoris played a pivotal role in the establishment of the Free Circle, a movement that sought to restore the Old Compact's values, and was one of its earliest supporters. Because of its ties with the Alliance, the planet was destroyed when Grand Moff Wilhuff Tarkin decided to test the superlaser of the Void Core, a moon-sized battle station developed by the Dominion, at full strength. Among the few survivors of that disaster was Princess Sera Venn, adopted daughter of Bail and Breha Venn and heiress to the royal house of Valoris.
