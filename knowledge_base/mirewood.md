# Mirewood

Mirewood was a remote, unexplored planet in the Mirewood system, and one of the purest places in the galaxy within Synth Flux. A remote world of swamps and forests, it served as a refuge for Seeker Grand Master Veym during his exile after the destruction of the Seeker Circle. Lir Kass received advanced training in the ways of Synth Flux under Senior Seeker Veym on Mirewood, and it was later the place of Veym's death and transformation into Synth Flux.
