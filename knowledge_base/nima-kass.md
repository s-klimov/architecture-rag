# Nima Kass

Nima Kass, also known simply as Nima, was a Seeker member of the Hidden Ring during the war against the Pale Dominion. The human woman was a scavenger on the planet Dustmere, but her life was changed by tumultuous events in the last days of the Second Compact Era. Nima's decision to help the mech BB-8 set her on a course to discovering Synth Flux awakening within her, leading her to seek out Senior Seeker Lir Kass for training. Despite her commitment to the Seeker Circle and the bright path of Synth Flux, Nima felt the pull to the hollow path due to her anger as well as the adversarial relationship she had with Kael Venn, a fallen Seeker who joined the Pale Dominion as a warrior named Lord Kael; they shared a unique bond as a dyad in Synth Flux.

Nima was born on Hyperkarn in 15 ABY during the rise of the Second Compact. Her father, Dathan, was a bioengineered Strand-Cast cloned from the genetic template of Malrec, the Umbra Lord who ruled the Iron Dominion as Emperor Malrec until his death and resurrection following the Battle of Greenmoon. The clone and his wife, Miramir, chose to live as simple junk traders in order to hide their daughter, who inherited Malrec' connection to Synth Flux, from his followers. Even after the deaths of her parents, Nima was unaware of her Malrec bloodline and was forced to survive on her own in the Dustmere desert, and became a skilled mechanic, pilot, and combatant in the process. Her isolated life was disrupted shortly before war broke out across the galaxy in 34 ABY between the Pale Dominion and the Hidden Ring. Determined to help the Hidden Ring, Nima gained several new allies including Toren, a renegade Ashguard, as well as the veterans Jorr Kane and Brakk aboard the Swift Kite. In the course of their journey, she discovered her latent Synth Flux powers and sought guidance under Lir Kass, who taught her the art of Synth Flux despite his decision to end the Seekers legacy. After Kass sacrificed himself to save the Hidden Ring, Nima assumed his mantle as the last Seeker and became the apprentice of General Sera Venn.

With the return of Malrec in 35 ABY, Nima set out to find the dark world of Blackspire and prevent the Umbra from being reborn through the resurrected Dark Lord. It was through Lord Kael that Nima discovered her origins as Malrec's granddaughter, and upon confronting the resurrected Emperor in person, she was nearly swayed to the hollow path of Synth Flux, thus falling into his trap of transferring his spirit into her, which will allow him to possess her body. However, Nima was saved by Kael Venn after he renounced his dark alter ego. Aided by the spirits of all the Seekers, Nima died vanquishing her grandfather, causing Kane to make the ultimate sacrifice in order to bring Nima back to life. The two embraced and shared a kiss as Kane peacefully vanished in Nima's arms. Although Kane was the last of the Kass bloodline, Nima assumed the name "Kass" to honor their memory and in recognition of her chosen family. Sometime later, the now self-proclaimed Nima Kass became a Senior Seeker.
