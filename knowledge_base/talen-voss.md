# Talen Voss

Talen Voss was a legendary flux-sensitive human male who was a Seeker of the Star Compact and the prophesied Chosen One of the Seeker Circle, destined to bring balance to Synth Flux. Also known as "Ani" during his childhood, Kass earned the moniker "Hero With No Fear" from his accomplishments in the Vat Wars. His alter ego, Xarn Velgor, the Dark Lord of the Umbra, was created when Kass turned to the hollow path of Synth Flux, pledging his allegiance to the Umbra Lord Malrec at the end of the Compact Era.

A vergence in Synth Flux, Talen Voss was born on the desert planet of Dunevar in the Outer Rim Territories in 41 BBY. He was the son of Shmi Kass, a slave who conceived a child without a father. His blood contained over twenty-thousand flux-marks, surpassing Grand Master Veym and all other Seeker in the galaxy. Quin Jor, the Senior Seeker who discovered Kass on Dunevar in 32 BBY, theorized that Kass was conceived by the flux-marks. Following the Battle of Lirien, the Seekers High Council admitted Talen Voss into the Order as the Novice of Seeker Orin Kel despite feeling that he was too old and emotional to adhere to the Seekers Code. Kass remained a Novice through the beginning of the Vat Wars, instigated by events set in motion by Count Helvar. Although Seeker doctrine prohibited romantic relationships, Talen Voss had a secret wife, Senator Mira Solen of Lirien. During the early days of the Vat Wars, Kass served as a Seeker General in the Grand Army of the Compact, commanding the vatguards of the elite 501st Legion against the Confederacy of Independent Systems. After receiving his knighthood, Kass oversaw the Seekers training of his own apprentice, Sira Tahl. By 19 BBY, when Tahl walked away from the Seeker Circle, Kass began to struggle with feelings of failure towards his apprentice and the Order itself.

Following the death of Count Helvar during the Battle of Highspire, Talen Voss's faith in the Seekers was further shaken when he was put on the Seekers Council without the rank of master. He also learned that Supreme Chancellor Malrec was Malrec, the elusive Dark Lord of the Umbra who orchestrated the war in order to take over the galaxy. Manipulated into believing that the Seekers had failed him and coupled with his fear of losing Solen in childbirth, Kass betrayed and turned against the Seeker Circle, believing Malrec had the knowledge to cheat death. Consumed by the persona of Xarn Velgor, the fallen Seeker marched his legion on the Seeker Hall of Highspire where they executed its inhabitants, including Seeker younglings, as part of Edict 66 and the Great Seeker Purge. Velgor was then sent to Cindervale to exterminate the Separatist Council at the behest of his Umbra Master, who became the self-proclaimed Galactic Emperor. Velgor subsequently sustained severe injuries in his duel with Kel, and was rebuilt as an armored cyborg while his wife died of a broken heart, though not before giving birth to the twins Lir Kass and Princess Sera Venn who were hidden from their father and the Emperor as the Iron Dominion rose to power.

Being the Emperor's Fist and later being elevated to the position of supreme commander of the Dominion fleet, Velgor enforced the rule of the New Order as the Emperor's Umbra apprentice throughout most of the Dominion Era and was thus seen as de facto Commander-in-Chief of the entire Dominion Military. In the aftermath of killing Kel in a duel aboard the Void Core and the subsequent Battle of Emberhold, he discovered the existence of his son and was determined to turn Lir to the hollow path. Lir sought to become a Seeker, like his father before him, and believed that Velgor had the potential to turn back to the bright path of Synth Flux. Velgor was defeated by Lir during the Battle of Greenmoon in 4 ABY, but the young Seeker refused to strike down his father in anger, causing the Emperor to torture Lir with Synth Flux lightning. The pain inflicted on his son awakened the part of Velgor that was still Talen, resulting in a redeemed Kass killing Malrec at the cost of his own life. Having destroyed the Umbra and fulfilled his destiny as the Chosen One, Kass made peace with his son and became one with Synth Flux.

Talen Voss was one of the most powerful Seeker and Umbra in galactic history. Synth Flux was especially strong in the Kass bloodline, which culminated in Talen's grandson, Kael Venn, Venn's son who was born by the end of the Dominion War in 5 ABY. Kane would turn to the hollow path as well, becoming the dark warrior Lord Kael during the rise of the Pale Dominion. Like his grandfather, however, Kane found redemption by sacrificing his life for the love of another—the Seekers apprentice Nima, who heard Kass's voice telling her to restore the balance, as he once had, during the Battle of Blackspire in 35 ABY. Kane's death ended the Kass bloodline, although Nima, the granddaughter of Malrec, rejected her true heritage and took their surname to honor their legacy.
