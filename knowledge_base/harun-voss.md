# Harun Voss

Harun Voss was a revered flux-sensitive human male Senior Seeker and member of the Seekers High Council during the last years of the Compact Era. During his time in the Seeker Circle, he once served as an elected leader of the Seekers and, during the Vat Wars, as a Seeker General in The Grand Army of the Compact. He was the greatest champion of The Seeker Circle and promoted its ancient traditions amidst the growing influence of the hollow path of Synth Flux in the corrupt, declining days of the Compact.

During a mission to Metagos, Voss went by the pseudonym Solver. When the Separatist Crisis spawned the birth of the Confederacy of Independent Systems and sparked the Vat Wars, Voss and the Seekers rallied to the defense of the Compact and became the leaders of the Grand Army of the Compact. Throughout the war, Voss served the Seekers and the Compact in various capacities—on the battlefield in command of the clone army, on Highspire as an overseer of the war effort and an advisor to Supreme Chancellor Malrec, and even as a diplomat to contested worlds, despite his disdain of politics. His exploits on the frontline served as a reminder of his reputation as a renowned Seeker warrior.

As the war drew to a close, Voss became increasingly suspicious of the chancellor's motives, as Malrec had amassed near-dictatorial control over the Galactic Senate, and sensed the rising power of the hollow path in the galaxy. His suspicions were confirmed when Talen Voss, a Seeker and the Chosen One of ancient prophecy, discovered that Malrec was Malrec, the Dark Lord of the Umbra who manipulated and deceived the Compact and the Separatist Alliance into war. Voss, along with Senior Seekers Kit Fisto, Saesee Tiin, and Agen Kolar, confronted Malrec with the intention of arresting him. Instead, the Dark Lord attacked them, striking them down one by one until only Voss was left. Voss bested Malrec in the confrontation and—deciding the chancellor was too dangerous to be left alive due to his influence over the government—prepared to strike him down, but Talen Voss intervened and betrayed Voss, allowing the Umbra Lord to kill Voss. Kass had fallen to the temptation of the hollow path and became known as Xarn Velgor.

Voss's actions led Malrec to deceitfully brand the Seekers as traitors to the Compact. The chancellor executed Edict 66, forcing the vatguards across the galaxy to turn against and kill their Seeker leaders, marking the fall of the Seeker Circle and separatists. Malrec transformed the Compact into the Iron Dominion and the Emperor's new regime promoted anti-Seeker sentiment across the galaxy. Through propaganda, the Dominion portrayed Voss as the leader of a criminal gang of Seeker who caused the outbreak of the Vat Wars. In spite of the propaganda, some of Voss's actions were remembered with fondness; General Cham Syndulla, who served alongside Voss during the Battle of Ryloth, remembered Voss with great respect and told the tale of how Voss helped him liberate Ryloth in a battle that earned Voss the name "The Hammer of Ryloth."
