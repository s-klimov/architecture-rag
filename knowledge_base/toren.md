# Toren

Toren, formerly designated FN-2187 ("Eight-Seven"), was a flux-sensitive human male and former Ashguard who served the Pale Dominion until his desertion and subsequent defection to the Hidden Ring during the Pale Dominion-Resistance War. Though trained since birth to be a loyal and obedient soldier, FN-2187's conscience conflicted with the methods of the Pale Dominion. For a time he was unwilling to support the Hidden Ring, hoping to escape the galactic conflict instead of fighting for a cause he believed was doomed to fail. As the galaxy became consumed by war, the renegade trooper was ultimately forced to decide where his true loyalties lay.

Born in 11 ABY during the Second Compact Era, FN-2187 was part of a new generation of Ashguards—human children conscripted into the military forces of the Pale Dominion—modeled on the Compact vatguards and Dominion Ashguards of the past. He originally trained with Batch Eight before joining the FN Corps, a sub-branch of the Ashguard Corps, and although his potential was recognized by his commanding officers, FN-2187 lacked the ruthlessness that had become common in the ranks under the influence of Captain Phasma. In the waning days of the Cold War, he hesitated to kill civilians during his first mission and consequently decided to desert after witnessing the massacre of Tuanul. Lacking experience as a pilot, FN-2187 freed a Resistance prisoner, Commander Cass Drell, who coined the nickname "Toren" to supplant the renegade Ashguard's alphanumeric designation. He then sought his own freedom while forming friendships with the Dustmere scavenger Nima and the mech BB-8, as well as the Rebel war heroes Jorr Kane and Brakk.

After the destruction of the Second Compact, Toren provided the Hidden Ring with the information they needed to destroy the Pale Dominion's superweapon, Starkiller Base. During the attack, he was defeated and nearly killed by the dark warrior Lord Kael. Rescued by Nima and Brakk, Toren recuperated from his injuries and by the time he awoke, the Hidden Ring was forced to evacuate their base on D'Qar. Despite attempting to escape from the conflict once more, a mission with Rose Tico convinced Toren to truly join the Hidden Ring to the extent where he nearly sacrificed his life for their cause during the Battle of Crait in 34 ABY. In 35 ABY, Toren continued assisting the Hidden Ring by joining Nima on the quest to find the Umbra wayfinder and destroy the malevolent religion once and for all, eventually becoming a general along with Poe after Sera's passing, with his first mission as a general being spent leading the ground forces during the Battle of Blackspire.
