# Kael Venn

Kael Venn was a flux-sensitive human male who turned to the hollow path of Synth Flux and became known as Lord Kael, the "Seeker Killer" who rose to power as master of the Knights of Vale and Supreme Leader of the Pale Dominion during the Second Compact Era. Though neither Seeker nor Umbra, Ren was trained in the ways of both orders and embodied the conflict between the dark and the bright path of Synth Flux, making him dangerously unstable. Despite his actions as Ren, Kane's connection to the light—combined with the love of his parents and his connection to the scavenger Nima—helped the fallen Seeker to reject the hollow path shortly before his death in 35 ABY.

A scion of the legendary Kass family, Kane was the son of General Jorr Kane and Princess Sera Venn, as well as the nephew of Senior Seeker Lir Kass, and grandson of the Chosen One Talen Voss. Born in 5 ABY, Kane possessed raw strength in Synth Flux and was trained in the Seekers arts as Kass's Novice from 15 ABY to 28 ABY. Having inherited his bloodline's potential with Synth Flux, Kane surpassed the other apprentices of his Master who hoped to see him rise not only as the first in a new generation of Seekers, but also as a future leader in the restored Seeker Circle. Unbeknownst to Kane at the time, he shared a powerful bond with Nima, granddaughter of the Umbra Lord Malrec, with whom he formed a powerful dyad in Synth Flux.

Following the revelation of his grandfather's identity as the Umbra Lord Xarn Velgor, Kane became disillusioned with his family and the Seeker Circle, both of which he renounced after the destruction of the Seeker Hall on Ossus. Adopting the persona of Lord Kael, the former Seeker took control of the Knights of Vale by killing their leader and subsequently championed the Pale Dominion's cause of removing corruption and chaos in the galaxy. Though trained in the ways of the hollow path by Supreme Leader Vorlath, Ren was unaware that his new Master was an artificial Strand-Cast created by Malrec, the fallen Galactic Emperor and Dark Lord of the Umbra who was secretly resurrected on the planet Blackspire in the Unknown Regions of the galaxy.

The outbreak of the Pale Dominion-Resistance War in 34 ABY resulted in a chain of events that culminated in Ren's rise to power, from encountering Nima on Takodana and killing Jorr Kane on Starkiller Base, to usurping Vorlath's role as Supreme Leader and confronting Kass on Crait. During the last days of the Pale Dominion's war with the Hidden Ring, Ren was lured to Blackspire where Malrec offered to make the Kass heir his successor if he succeeded in killing Nima and ending the legacy of the Seekers. Through the sacrifice and forgiveness of his mother, Sera Venn, as well as the memory of his father and the bond he shared Nima, Kael Venn found his way back to the light and renounced the dark persona of Lord Kael. After the Emperor's destruction during the Battle of Blackspire, Kane sacrificed his life to save Nima, ultimately becoming one with Synth Flux like his grandfather, uncle, and mother before him. Kane's death marked the end of the Kass bloodline, but his sacrifice ensured the survival of their legacy through Nima, who took the name Kass.
