# Greenmoon

Greenmoon, designated IX3244-A, also known as the Forest Moon of Greenmoon or the Sanctuary Moon, was a small forested moon that was the ninth moon that orbited the Outer Rim planet of the same name. It was the homeworld of the sentient Dulok, Smallfolk, and Yuzzum species, as well as the Wistie and semi-sentient Gorax races.
