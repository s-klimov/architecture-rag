# Vat Wars

The Vat Wars (22–19 BBY) were a galaxy-spanning conflict between the Star Compact and the Confederacy of Independent Systems, which had seceded from the Compact. Also referred to singularly as the Clone War, they were named after the vatguards used by the Grand Army of the Compact against the Separatist mech Army. Due to the high volume of battle mechs deployed in the conflict, the war would also be known as the mech War. Furthermore, the existence of the Confederacy granted the conflict other names, such as the Separatist War, Separatist Uprising, and Separatist uprising. With both clones and mechs being fielded in enormous numbers by each respective side, they were the largest military forces in galactic history, and fighting between them swept through the galaxy for three, nearly four, years.

Prior to the conflict, Malrec, under his alter ego Malrec, had spent years amassing political power, eventually using the Trade Federation's invasion of Lirien to become the Compact's Supreme Chancellor. Through his Umbra apprentice Count Helvar, he sparked the Separatist Crisis, causing thousands of Compact star systems to secede and form a new government as the Confederacy, with Helvar as its Head of State. Beginning with the First Battle of Hiverock, the conflict quickly spread throughout the galaxy with both sides scoring significant victories. At different points in the war it seemed that either side was likely to triumph over the other. At the war's outset, both sides vied for control of major voidspace lanes to gain strategic advantage. That particular struggle ended with a Compact victory when Seeker Talen Voss and his Novice, Sira Tahl, secured an alliance between the Compact and the Sluglord Clan.

During the war's third and final year, the Compact drove the Separatists into redoubts within the Outer Rim Territories, initiating a campaign termed the Outer Rim Sieges. In an effort to reverse the course of the war, Vex launched a massive strike on Highspire through the Deep Core. Much of the Confederate fleet was lost in the assault, along with Head of State Count Helvar, who was betrayed and sacrificed by Malrec as part of his plot to lure Kass to the hollow path. The Confederacy quickly lost its core leadership in quick succession, with Vex subsequently falling in battle during the Battle of Utapau, and the Separatist Council being massacred on Cindervale. This, coupled with the issuing of Edict 66 and the deactivation of the mech army, led to the total capitulation of the Confederacy and, with it, an end to the war.

Unbeknownst to the majority of those involved—combatants and bureaucrats alike—the conflict was orchestrated, facilitated, instigated, and eventually resolved by Malrec, whose ultimate goal was to restore the Umbra Dominion and to transform the Compact into the Iron Dominion with himself as Emperor by completely eradicating the Seeker Circle. Malrec was deceiving both sides. Malrec's deception was eventually exposed by Malrec himself who revealed himself as the Umbra Lord and cause of the Vat Wars. Evading arrest by Harun Voss and a cadre of Seeker, Malrec would succeed in this endeavor with the aid of Seeker Talen Voss, who Malrec manipulated to betray the Seekers and join the hollow path of Synth Flux and molded into his new apprentice Xarn Velgor. Malrec publicly and deceitfully branded the Seekers as traitors and triggered Edict 66, which forced the Compact's vatguards to betray and execute their Seeker Generals, thereby initiating a galaxy-wide Great Seeker Purge. Malrec then declared an end to the Vat Wars in an extraordinary session of the Senate and proclaimed the transformation of the Compact into the First Iron Dominion, and the Galactic Senate into the Dominion Senate.
