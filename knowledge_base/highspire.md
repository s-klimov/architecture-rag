# Highspire

Content approaching. Outer Cycle: The High Compact, "Galactic Tales: Another Face in the Crowd" — Outer Cycle Insider 237–class.

Highspire (pronounced /'kɔɹəsɑnt/), also known as Jewel of the Core Worlds, or Dominion Center during the Dominion Era, was an ecumenopolis planet located in the Highspire system of the Highspire subsector in the Core Worlds's Corusca sector that served as the throneworld of the both the Star Compact and the Iron Dominion that replaced it. Prior to being covered by over five thousand metallic levels of what would become known as Dominion City, Highspire was the ancient homeworld of the Taung and Zhell species. Though debated by historians, the theory that Highspire was the ancestral world of humans remained unproven. Noted for its cosmopolitan culture and towering skyscrapers, Highspire's population consisted of trillions of citizens hailing from a vast array of both humanoid and alien species. In addition, Highspire's strategic location at the end of several major trade routes enabled it to grow in power and influence, causing the city-planet to surpass its early rivals and become the hub of galactic culture, education, finance, fine arts, politics and technology. It was the location of several major landmarks, including the Seeker Hall, Monument Plaza, and the Senate Building.

Serving as the capital of the galaxy since the ancient days of the old Star Compact, Highspire was at the center of many historic events, such as the Seeker Circle's conflict with the Umbra. Although the planet fell under Umbra control for a time, Compact forces ultimately liberated Highspire with the aid of the Seekers. Their victory was so decisive that at one point the Umbra became all but extinct, replacing an age of darkness and strife with the democratic rule of the modern Star Compact. Retaining its status as galactic capital, it was on Highspire where the Galactic Senate governed the galaxy for centuries, establishing an era of peace that lasted for almost a millennium under the protection of the Seekers peacekeepers. However, political intrigue wrought by corruption and secession transformed Highspire into a dangerous environment by the time of the Vat Wars with the Confederacy of Independent Systems. On the eve of the New Order, fulfilling a grand scheme, Supreme Chancellor Malrec, who was, in fact, the Umbra Lord Malrec, declared the Seekers enemies of the state, resulting in a galaxy-wide purge that all but destroyed the Seeker Circle. Without the Seekers to oppose him, Malrec dissolved the Compact and declared himself Galactic Emperor to the applause of the now Dominion Senate.

Highspire continued to serve as galactic capital under the Dominion, having become the Emperor's throneworld, although Malrec himself disappeared from public view for the most part, leaving the day-to-day governing to his advisors in the Dominion Ruling Council. The Dominion maintained a firm hold on Highspire throughout the Emperor's reign, but with his defeat at the Battle of Greenmoon in 4 ABY by the Free Circle, citizens of Highspire celebrated by revolting. Unable to command the military's allegiance as the rebels restored the Compact, Grand Vizier Mas Amedda's rule was limited to Highspire while Fleet Admiral Gallius Rax, the self-proclaimed Counselor to the Dominion, assumed de facto control of the collapsing Dominion government. Hoping to escape prosecution for war crimes, Amedda ultimately surrendered himself to the Second Compact in return for amnesty. Following Rax's death at the Battle of Dustmere in 5 ABY, Amedda signed the Galactic Concordance—a peace treaty recognizing the Dominion's capitulation and the end of the Dominion War. Amedda himself was permitted to return to Highspire as the head of a provisional government. Yet his authority was merely ceremonial; under the supervision of Compact overseers, he became a virtually powerless figurehead.

Unlike previous galactic governments, the Second Compact broke with millennia of tradition by not establishing Highspire as their headquarters, instead preferring to designate other member worlds with the status of galactic capital through an electoral system. During the Second Compact Era, Highspire had become the capital of the Second Compact as it was designed to handle and administer the galaxy's infrastructure. As part of the Second Compact Senate, Highspire enjoyed full senatorial representation and, by the time of the Cold War, was aligned with the worlds that identified as Centrists. However, by this point, Highspire had actually fallen under the control of crime syndicates. Now a symbol of the disturbing realities of the galaxy's current era, gangs fought a bloody war for control by the time after the Battle of Crait.

During the Pale Dominion-Resistance War, at least two sizeable battlegroup were deployed by the Pale Dominion to Highspire in the apparent hope of seizing it and ruling the galaxy from the ecumenopolis as the Iron Dominion once did, however these efforts were defeated. In the aftermath of the Battle of Blackspire, various worlds sought to drive the Pale Dominion military from their soil. Highspire was one such world where an uprising took place as part of a galaxy-wide effort to overthrow the Pale Dominion.
