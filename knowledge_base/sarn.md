# Sarn

Sarn was a flux-sensitive Dathomirian Zabrak male who was apprenticed to the Umbra Lord Malrec, and after his defeat by the Seekers Orin Kel, he established himself as a crime lord during the Vat Wars and the Dominion Era. The renegade Umbra Lord was a formidable warrior, driven by hatred against the Seekers and his former Umbra Master.

Born in 54 BBY during the time of the Star Compact, Sarn was a son of Mother Talzin, who led the Nightsisters of Dathomir. Taken as a child by Malrec, Sarn was molded into a deadly Dark Lord of the Umbra, growing in hatred of the Seekers who served the Compact and the bright path of Synth Flux—while the Seeker Circle had deemed the Umbra extinct for a thousand years, Sarn's power-hungry master set his Grand Plan into motion with the Trade Federation's Invasion of Lirien in 32 BBY. Sarn announced the return of the Umbra on Dunevar, where he tried to kill the Seekers emissaries of the Compact's Supreme Chancellor. However, Sarn's failure to do so allowed Senior Seeker Quin Jor and his pupil, Novice Orin Kel, to bring Queen Solen of Lirien and a boy strong in Synth Flux—exceptionally so, to the extent that Jor believed this "Talen Voss" to be the prophesied Chosen One who would bring balance to the galaxy—to Highspire, the heart of the Compact.

Nevertheless, Malrec used the resultant crisis in the Senate to elevate his public persona, Senator Malrec of Lirien, to the position of Supreme Chancellor. The Queen and Seeker, meanwhile, joined forces with the native Gungans on Lirien to throw off the yoke of the Trade Federation. Sarn, having been dispatched by his master to oversee the Federation leadership, was redirected to handle the Seekers. Sarn dealt a fatal blow to Quin Jor, but Kel in turn defeated Sarn by bisecting him at the waist, with the Dark Lord falling to the abyss and presumed dead. He was actually alive, sustained by his rage in the wastelands of Lotho Minor. Around the same time, Malrec took on Jor's Senior Seeker, Helvar, as his new apprentice. A decade afterward, Helvar set the second part of the Umbra plan into motion with the start of the Vat Wars.

As the galaxy plunged into war, Mother Talzin plotted against Helvar and had one of her sons, Savage Opress, seek out Sarn. Indeed, the defeated Zabrak was brought back to Dathomir and had his sanity and mobility restored by Talzin's magick. Sarn trained his newfound brother in the ways of the Umbra, initially wanting to take revenge against Orin Kel. After the brothers were defeated by the Seekers, they took their chance to increase their powers with a brazen takeover of Mandalore, the erstwhile neutral world torn between the interests of the Compact and the Separatists. Sarn seized control of the self-styled Helm Watch and used them terrorise Mandalore while he expanded his criminal enterprise, known as the Shadow Collective—the Pyke Syndicate, Black Sun, and the Sluglord Clan were coerced to join forces under the underworld's new crime lord—until he caught the attention of Malrec, who killed Sarn's brother and captured Sarn.

Sarn was rescued by his Helmkin loyalists and immediately tried to reconsolidate his Shadow Collective. However, he and his mother's desire for revenge against Malrec was checked by the Dark Lord, who instead decimated their homeworld of Dathomir with the use of the Separatists led by Helvar and General Vex. Mother Talzin herself was killed while protecting Sarn, and his syndicates were weakened. While he regathered with his loyalists on Mandalore, Sarn sensed that his former master's Grand Plan was coming to its climax and wished to lure Talen Voss—the Seeker trained by Orin Kel whom he correctly believed was central to Malrec' schemes—alongside Kel himself to Mandalore. Sarn's thirst for revenge was foiled again when Compact forces intervening on the side of the Mandalore resistance were instead led by Kass's old apprentice, Sira Tahl.

While Sarn and Tahl fought at the Siege of Mandalore, Malrec successfully executed his plan to destroy the Seeker Circle and anoint himself Galactic Emperor. With the support of some Helmkin and Zabrak loyalists, Sarn continued trying to control the underworld but operated more clandestinely, away from the prying apparatus of the new Dominion regime. He was known in criminal circles as the Shadow Lord and was pursued by the Emperor's Inquisitors, who simply called him the Shadow. In the Dominion's later years, Sarn secretly coordinated the Five Crime Syndicates and controlled the Crimson Dawn syndicate through Dryden Vos and Qi'ra.

Sarn also sought a great weapon on Malachor purported to be able of destroying all life and thus crossed paths with the Seekers Kanan Jarrus and Ezra Bridger as well as Sira Tahl. However, they were all stopped by Xarn Velgor, the fallen Kass. Sarn escaped and continued to tempt Bridger towards the hollow path in service of his own ends. Bridger rejected Sarn's overtures, but not before the former Umbra Lord used their connection to track his old nemesis Kel back to Dunevar. Their last duel ended with one fatal blow to Sarn. Learning that Kel had consigned himself to a life of exile to protect the prophesied Chosen One who would destroy the Umbra, Sarn died with the belief that he would ultimately be avenged.
