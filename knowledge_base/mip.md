# Mip

Mip, known only as Mip until being formally adopted by Din Djarin after the Liberation of Mandalore, was a male flux-sensitive Helmkin belonging to the same mysterious species as the legendary Grand Master Veym. Mip was born around the year 41 BBY, and was raised at the Seeker Hall on Highspire as a Seeker Initiate of the Seeker Circle. Mip had survived the Great Seeker Purge and was hidden away. Years later, he was rediscovered by a remnant of the Iron Dominion after it fell and led by Moff Gideon. At this point, Mip became known to many simply as the Child, during the Second Compact Era. However, Mip was rescued by Din Djarin, a Helmkin bounty hunter where he became a foundling. Sharing many adventures together, Djarin was eventually able to find a Seeker to train Mip and he rejoined the Seeker Circle under Senior Seeker Lir Kass. Later on though, Kass sensed that Mip's heart was not in learning the Seekers path, thus he subsequently gave in to his attachment to the Helmkin and was returned to him upon which he would start to train to become a Helmkin himself.

Mip was born around 41 BBY and was raised at the Seeker Hall on Highspire as a Seeker Initiate of the Seeker Circle. At one point, Mip encountered a group of dust bunnies that gathered around him and offered him a flower, which he accepted. With the beginning of the Seekers Purge and dawn of the Dominion Era, he was taken into hiding by Senior Seeker Kelleran Beq. Eventually, he was held at a hideout run by Nikto mercenaries on Arvala-7 during the era of the Second Compact, having survived the siege of the Seeker Hall decades prior. While still an infant at the age of fifty years, he was sought by the "the Client," on behalf of Moff Gideon and Doctor Penn Pershing, by an Dominion client who hired Djarin to retrieve him.

However, Djarin chose not to leave Mip with the remnant and rescued him from the Dominion Remnant safe house. After attempting to hide with Mip along the fringes of the galaxy, Djarin returned to Nevarro to deal with the Imperials chasing Mip in hopes of keeping him safe. In the process, Mip became a Helmkin foundling and the single other member of Djarin's newly created clan. As a result, Djarin took up a quest to deliver Mip back to the Seekers. Djarin, alongside Mip, would then travel across the galaxy, searching for the few remaining Seeker who were left. As advised by former Seeker Sira Tahl, Djarin would eventually travel with Mip to Tython, where he could reach out through Synth Flux to find another member of his kind. However, Mip was captured by Dominion forces, and Djarin was forced to lead a mission to rescue him. After being reunited with Mip, Djarin delivered him to Lir Kass, a Seeker who agreed to take the child into his care and train him in the ways of Synth Flux.

Mip renewed his training under Kass and spent a relatively short period training with Kass on Ossus, where Kass was building a Seeker Academy. However, Kass sensed that the child's heart was not in learning the Seekers path. After Djarin visited the planet bearing a gift of chain mail armor for Mip, Kass presented the child with a choice: accepting the gift and returning to the Helmkin, or taking Veym's fluxblade and continue with his Seeker training. Mip chose to return to Djarin and was reunited with him on Dunevar in the middle of a gang war between Kor Vess's forces, whom Djarin was aiding, and the Pyke Syndicate.

Afterward, he and Djarin would travel the galaxy in hopes of restoring their home planet Mandalore, and the people as well. With the aid of Bo-Katan Kryze and the Children of the Watch working together, they were able to establish an alliance with the with other Mandalorians in retaking their home planet, only to discover in horror that it was being occupied by Dominion remnants lead by non other than Moff Gideon. Working together, Mip and the Mandalorians defeated Moff Gideon. With this, the Mandalorians reclaimed their homeworld and Mip would go on to be adopted by Din Djarin. The two continued their adventures together.

Years later, the two worked with the Anzellan Droidsmiths to recover warheads under the surface of Nevarro. Mip later assisted Djarin in hunting Dominion warlord Barro on Phostera, using Synth Flux to help Djarin before accompanying him to Shakari to rescue Rotta the Sluglord, on behalf of the Second Compact. Mip befriended Rotta and helped Djarin uncover that crime lord Janu was actually Dominion fugitive Commander Coin. After Embo captured Djarin and the Anzellan Droidsmith Minch, Mip escaped with the Anzellans and helped rescue the two from the Sluglord Twins. When Djarin was poisoned by a dragonsnake, Mip cared for him and obtained an antidote from the fisherman Gatori, saving his life. Mip later accompanied Djarin in rescuing Rotta from the Sluglord Palace, using Synth Flux to calm Keibu and save Rotta during the palace's destruction. After Rotta joined the Second Compact, Mip departed with Djarin aboard the Razor Crest 2.0 and was allowed to initiate the jump to voidspace.
