# Veym

Veym was a legendary Seeker Grand Master who led the Seeker Circle through the time of the High Compact, in the years leading up to its destruction by the Umbra, and during the transformation of the Star Compact into the Iron Dominion. Small in stature but revered for his wisdom and power, Veym trained generations of Seeker, ultimately serving as the Seeker Circle's Grand Master. He played an integral role in defending the Compact during the Vat Wars, survived Edict 66, lived to pass on the Seekers tradition to Lir Kass, and unlocked the path to immortality.

Veym was almost nine hundred years old in the latter days of the Compact. A leading member of the Seekers High Council, his contemporaries included other legendary Masters, such as Harun Voss and Ki-Adi-Mundi. During the Invasion of Lirien, in 32 BBY, the maverick Quin Jor introduced Talen Voss to the High Council, believing with absolute certainty that he had discovered the prophesied Chosen One. the Seekers elders sensed that the boy was full of fear and anger and declined to train him, deeming Kass too old and emotionally compromised to commit himself to the Seekers Code. After Jor's death during the liberation of Lirien, the High Council reversed their decision in spite of Veym's continued opposition to Kass's apprenticeship, having sensed grave danger in his training. At the same time, the Seekers discovered that their ancient nemesis, the Umbra, had returned after a millennium in hiding. Aware of the Rule of Two, Veym was convinced that at least one more Umbra Lord remained active following Orin Kel's victory over the Umbra apprentice Sarn.

Over the next decade, the galaxy was on the verge of civil war with entire star systems—led by Count Helvar, Veym's one-time Novice—threatening to secede from the Compact by 22 BBY. Unable to defend the entire Compact against this betrayal on their own, the Seekers took command of the newly-formed Grand Army of the Compact, with Veym himself leading an army of vatguards against the Separatist mech Army of the Confederacy of Independent Systems in the first battle of the Vat Wars, rescuing the Seekers and Senator from execution. For three years Veym led the Compact military's war effort as a Seeker General, determined to bring a swift and decisive end to the conflict. During this time he was approached by the spirit of Quin Jor, who helped Veym learn how to retain his identity as a spirit after death. Veym's efforts against the Separatists were undermined by Malrec, the Dark Lord of the Umbra who conspired to destroy the Seekers and restore the Umbra to power. After the Great Seeker Purge commenced in 19 BBY, killing thousands of Seeker both on Highspire and across the galaxy, Veym confronted the self-declared Galactic Emperor but failed to cut short Malrec' reign and consequently retreated into exile, leaving the Umbra Master to consolidate his power with a new apprentice, the former Talen Voss-turned-Xarn Velgor, at his side.

Veym's remaining years were spent living in isolation on the remote world of Mirewood. After the death of Orin Kel, he was able to commune with his spirit, who wished for Veym to train Talen's son Lir Kass. Veym initially reluctantly agreed, but later believed that the galaxy as better off without him and refused Kel's request. After being convinced by Kel to enter the Cave of Evil, in which he confronted many figures from his past and made peace with his failures, he agreed to teach Lir Kass, who soon after sought out the legendary Grand Master in the hope of becoming a Seeker. He trained Kass for a time, until despite Veym's warnings Kass hurriedly abandoned his training to save his friends after a having a vision that they were in danger. A year later, in 4 ABY, with his health rapidly declining, Veym died of old age when Kass returned to see him. He became one with Synth Flux, shortly before Lir redeemed his father Talen, who fulfilled his destiny as the Chosen One by killing Malrec.

Years later, Lir Kass's attempt to reform the Seeker Circle would be foiled when his nephew Kael Venn turned to the hollow path and destroyed his Seeker Hall. Believing the galaxy was better without him and the Seekers, Kass went into exile, just as Veym had. After his student he reluctantly trained, the scavenger Nima, also abandoned her training, Veym appeared as a ghost to Kass. Veym taught Kass to learn from his failures and helped him forgive himself.
