# Sira Tahl

Sira Tahl was a female Togruta who was a flux-sensitive outcast from the Seeker Circle. After the Vat Wars, she helped establish a network of various rebel cells against the Iron Dominion. Tahl was discovered on her homeworld of Shili by Senior Seeker Kel Vos, who brought her to the Seeker Hall on Highspire to receive Seeker training. Following the outbreak of the Vat Wars, Seeker Grand Master Veym assigned the young Tahl to be the Novice learner of Seeker Talen Voss, who nicknamed her "Snips" when she joined him at the Battle of Christophsis. Whereas Tahl was eager to prove herself, Kass had a reputation for recklessness, and they had a rather difficult start as master and apprentice. Yet they worked together to rescue Rotta, the son of crime lord Grull Desilijic Tiure, and returned Rotta to his father, thus facilitating a crucial alliance between the Sluglord Clan and the Star Compact.

Tahl gained much experience on and beyond the war front, leading a group of Seeker younglings to revolt against their Trandoshan captors, helping rescue a colony of Togruta from enslavement by the Zygerrian Slave Dominion, and serving as an advisor to the Onderon rebels—with her close friend, Lux Bonteri, among them—as they liberated their world from the Confederacy. In a stark turn of events, Tahl was framed for the bombing of the Seeker Hall hangar and other homicides, and she escaped into the Highspire Underworld to clear her name. Though she formed an unlikely alliance with Asajj Ventress, she was detained by Compact forces and was consequently barred from the Seeker Circle. Ultimately, Kass uncovered the true culprit, Tahl's friend, and fellow Novice Barriss Offee, and prevented his former apprentice from being convicted of sedition. Nevertheless, the ordeal dislodged Tahl's faith in the Seekers. She refused the Seekers High Council's offer to rejoin the Order, instead departing in search of a new path. Tahl briefly returned to lead Compact forces during the Siege of Mandalore and faced Sarn and his allies. Edict 66 was enacted shortly after the Umbra Lord's capture and Tahl alongside Rex, after freeing Rex from the control of Edict 66, escaped the rest of her 501st division. Faking her death, she was forced into hiding.

After the Iron Dominion came to power, Tahl went into hiding on Thabeska and Raada disguised as "Ashla." Following the evacuation of Raada, Tahl joined Senator Bail Venn's growing rebel movement. She became the manager of his intelligence network and adopted the codename "Fulcrum." As Fulcrum, Tahl provided intelligence to various rebel factions including Hera Syndulla's Spectres rebel cell. Tahl took a particular interest in Syndulla's rebels because one of their members, a Seeker named Kanan Jarrus, had begun to train his own Novice, Ezra Bridger. In addition, Tahl's efforts against the Dominion brought her into contact with the Dark Lord of the Umbra Xarn Velgor—who, unknown to her, was actually her former master, Talen Voss—as well as the Dominion Inquisitors known as the Fifth Brother and the Seventh Sister, but it also gave her the chance to reunite with Rex. Following the mission to Malachor, Tahl became lost to the rubble and shadows of the Umbra temple and was believed by many, including Jarrus and Bridger, to have perished during a duel with Xarn Velgor.

However, Tahl was unexpectedly saved from Velgor's wrath by a future Ezra Bridger, who pulled her through a portal into the World Between Worlds, a mystical plane of Synth Flux connecting all time and space. After a confrontation with Malrec, who desired access to the dimension, Tahl returned to her time, promising Bridger that she would find him once she returned. After the end of the Dominion War, Tahl joined Sabine Wren in her quest to find the missing Bridger, who had disappeared during the Liberation of Lothal, and trained Wren in the ways of the Seekers until parting ways. Tahl later journeyed to the city of Calodan on the forest planet Corvus looking for information on the location of Grand Admiral Thrawn, who had disappeared alongside Bridger, from Magistrate Morgan Elsbeth. Needing to rescue the city from Elsbeth's rule, she allied herself with the Helmkin Din Djarin, who had been on a quest to bring the flux-sensitive youngling Mip to the Seekers. After freeing Calodan, Tahl refused to train Mip and directed Djarin to the planet Tython instead. She later visited Lir Kass at his new Seeker Hall and, when Kass found Mip in his care following a summons from the seeing stone, oversaw his training of the child temporarily.

Faced with Thrawn's return, Tahl returned to Lothal and enlisted Wren's help once again in finding Bridger and the missing Grand Admiral. Decades after Tahl began her search, her voice joined a multitude of Seeker spirits to guide the young Seeker Nima, the granddaughter of a resurrected Malrec, during the final battle of the Pale Dominion-Resistance War. Sira inspired Nima to stand up to Malrec and destroy him once and for all.
