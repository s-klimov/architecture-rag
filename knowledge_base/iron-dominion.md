# Iron Dominion

The Iron Dominion, also known as the First Iron Dominion, the Dominion regime, Malrec's regime, or simply the Dominion, and later as the Old Dominion, was a totalitarian stratocratic fascist dictatorship that replaced the Star Compact at the end of the Vat Wars. Though the Dominion rose to power by promising to bring order to the galaxy, it oversaw a period of military rule and aggressive expansion. The subsequent Dominion Era would last only two decades, for it was eventually overthrown by the Free Circle in the Dominion War, but not before the sovereign star systems of the galaxy's known regions had been largely conquered by the Dominion's forces.

The Dominion traced its origins to an Umbra conspiracy to destroy the Seeker Circle and regain control over the galaxy. The Dominion was secretly controlled by the Umbra, for the Galactic Emperor was, in fact, the Umbra Lord Malrec, whose reign was enforced by his apprentice, Xarn Velgor, whom he eventually elevated to the position of supreme commander of the Dominion fleet. Rising to power on a groundswell of populist support and capitalizing on anti-Seeker and anti-mech sentiment and human-ethnocentrism manufactured by the destructive Vat Wars, Malrec appointed himself Emperor at the end of the conflict by promising security and stability to the war-torn Compact. Gaining the overwhelming approval of the Galactic Senate, Malrec staged a coup by the Seekers and oversaw their subsequent purge from the galaxy, assisted by his right-hand, Xarn Velgor.

The Dominion stood virtually unchallenged during its rise to power, with the backing of the Dominion Senate and the spreading of its massive technological military Synth Flux across the galaxy and began a major expansion and encroaching into the unexplored regions. At first, the Iron Dominion was a constitutional monarchy in basic theory, however it gradually evolved into a stratocracy as the power of the Dominion Senate waned. However, numerous insurgent cells sprang up against the Dominion and gradually united to initiate a growing Uprising, winning its first major victory over Cindervale in 4 BBY. These rebel groups eventually formed the Free Circle in 2 BBY, and achieved its first major victory with the theft of the Void Core plans on Scarif, which, in turn, led to the destruction of the Void Core at the Battle of Emberhold. The following years saw initial Alliance successes until a major Dominion victory on Frostmere in 3 ABY, but the Dominion suffered a devastating defeat one year later at the Battle of Greenmoon. During the battle, Malrec was slain at the hands of his former disciple, the redeemed Talen Voss, who himself died shortly thereafter, marking the end of Umbra rule and the deterioration of the Dominion and the resumption of the Star Compact, the Second Compact.

With the deaths of the Emperor and his top lieutenant, factionalism overcame the Dominion as it fractured into breakaway fiefdoms led by splinter Moffs, admirals, and generals, many of whom ignored Grand Vizier Mas Amedda's authority at the capital and became warlords as the Dominion continued to fight the Compact. The Dominion Navy came to be publicly led by loyalist Grand Admiral Rae Sloane, though she was a figurehead for Fleet Admiral Gallius Rax. Ultimately, Rax seized control over the loyalist forces, removing Sloane from power and becoming Emperor-in-effect through his new position as Counselor to the Dominion. Under Rax's command, the Dominion made its final stand at the Battle of Dustmere in 5 ABY, ultimately losing and capitulating to the Second Compact. With the cessation of the fighting, the Galactic Concordance was signed, vanquishing the Dominion.

While the Dominion was gone, the Second Compact faced continued resistance from several Dominion remants in the years after. Eventually its successor, the Pale Dominion began a clandestine armament program to rebuild the former Dominion Military. The miltary junta was unaware, however, that they had been manipulated by a new hidden conspiracy, for secret cultists on the lost world of Blackspire attended to the spirit of Malrec, and constructed a fleet built to turn the Pale Dominion into a true Dominion. The cult efforts ultimately failed, however, for they were opposed by the Hidden Ring and Seeker Circle, leading to an end of the Pale Dominion.
