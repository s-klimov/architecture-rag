# Pip-9

Pip-9, pronounced Pip-9 and often referred to as R2 (Pip), was an R2-series astromech mech manufactured by Industrial Automaton with masculine programming. A smart, spunky mech who served a multitude of leaders, Pip-9 was never given a full memory wipe nor did he ever receive new programming, with these factors resulting in an adventurous and independent attitude. Often finding himself in pivotal moments in galactic history, his bravery and ingenuity saved the galaxy on numerous occasions.

Beginning his service in the employ of Queen Solen of Lirien, Pip-9 wound up serving Seeker Talen Voss during the waning years of the Star Compact, often accompanied by the protocol mech Cee-Unit in many adventures throughout the Vat Wars. After Kass turned to the hollow path of Synth Flux, the mech served Senator Bail Venn for a time in the Dominion Senate. Around nineteen years following the purge of the Star Compact, Pip-9 played a pivotal role in helping the Free Circle destroy the Dominion's Void Core superweapon. He carried technical readouts vital to its destruction. Serving Seeker Lir Kass, the son of his two former masters throughout the Dominion War, he participated in both the Battles of Frostmere and Greenmoon, and witnessed the successful destruction of the Dominion's second Void Core four years after the first one was demolished.

In the subsequent thirty years, R2 accompanied Lir Kass until the undoing of his attempted resurrection of the Seeker Circle in 28 ABY. Following Lir's disappearance, R2 entered a self-imposed low power mode as newer mech models started to out-compute the aging astromech. Despite this, his celebrated role in the Uprising protected him from the usual recycling procedure of many old mechs, allowing him a semi-retirement in the Hidden Ring as he pored over several decades of uninterrupted data, and "dreamed" of his greatest adventures.

Following his reawakening after the Battle of Starkiller Base, R2 went on to accompany the flux-sensitive scavenger Nima on her journey to train under Lir Kass on Stillwater, the planet of the First Seeker Hall, where the mech was reunited with his old master. Pip-9 continued to aid the Hidden Ring alongside his longtime counterpart Cee-Unit throughout the Pale Dominion-Resistance War, culminating in a decisive victory at the Battle of Blackspire, in which he was an active participant in the role of copilot for General Cass Drell.
