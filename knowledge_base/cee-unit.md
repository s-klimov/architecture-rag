# Cee-Unit

Cee-Unit (Cee-Unit) was a 3PO-series protocol mech designed to interact with organics, programmed primarily for etiquette and protocol. Sometimes referred to as Cee, he was fluent in over six million forms of communication, and developed a fussy and worry-prone personality throughout his many decades of operation. Along with his counterpart, the astromech mech Pip-9, Cee-Unit constantly found himself directly involved in pivotal moments of galactic history, and aided in saving the galaxy on many occasions.

Cee-Unit was rebuilt from spare parts by Talen Voss, an enslaved human who lived in Mos Espa, a city on the Outer Rim world of Dunevar. During the Vat Wars, he would serve the Lirien Senator Mira Solen. Shortly after the Vat Wars, Cee-Unit's memory was erased, though Pip-9's memory was not. Cee-Unit and Pip-9 were assigned to the Valoris cruiser Tantive IV, where they served senator Bail Venn for nineteen years, assisting in his fledgling resistance movement against the Iron Dominion. At some point during this time, Cee-Unit's right leg was fitted with mismatched plating.

Cee-Unit remained constant companions with R2, and in 1 BBY, the pair became embroiled in the Dominion War when Sera Venn of the Free Circle entrusted them to bring a copy of the Void Core plans to the Senior Seeker Orin Kel on Dunevar. After meeting Kass's son, Lir and the smuggler Jorr Kane, the mechs helped rescue Princess Sera from the Dominion's Void Core. They became directly attached to the three humans, helping them and their Uprising defeat the Dominion and restore freedom to the galaxy, most notably when he convinced the Smallfolk to aid the Rebels at the Battle of Greenmoon.

Following the Battle of Greenmoon, Cee-Unit continued serving the Kass line and Kane during the time of the Second Compact. About thirty years after the events on Greenmoon, Cee-Unit was still an aide to Venn, who had become a general in the Hidden Ring, a private military group opposed to the Pale Dominion, a military junta that emulated the Old Dominion. By that time, Cee-Unit was fitted with a new set of gold plating, which had a more symmetrical design than his previous coverings, with the exception of his head. His new coverings included a third finger joint, and a torso that was not as slim as it used to be. Because of his new plating, he no longer had a mismatched right lower leg. Although, he did end up wearing a red left arm for a very short time. By that time, Cee-Unit's language database had also been updated so that he could understand and communicate in over seven million different forms of communication, including ancient Umbra dialects. He would assist Nima in translating Umbra writings in order to locate Malrec and the Umbra Eternal's fleet, the Final Order, on Blackspire, allowing his memory to be wiped in order to carry out the task. However, his friend R2 backed up a large portion of his memories, and after the Hidden Ring defeated Malrec and the Umbra Eternal in the Battle of Blackspire, his memory was restored.
