# Mira Solen

Mira Solen, also simply known as Mira Solen, was a human female politician who represented the people of Lirien during the final years of the Star Compact. Prior to her career as a senator in the Galactic Senate, Solen was the elected ruler of the Royal House of Lirien. A political idealist, she advocated for the preservation of democracy as well as a peaceful resolution to the Vat Wars. However, her secret marriage to the Seeker Talen Voss would have a lasting effect on the future of the galaxy for decades to come.

Born in 46 BBY during the Age of the Compact, Padmé Naberrie was raised by her parents, Ruwee Naberrie and his wife Jobal, on the peaceful Mid Rim world of Lirien. Despite the humble background of the Naberrie family, Padmé was identified early for her potential in public service. Devoting her life to civic duty, she was elected queen and, therefore, adopted the regnal name "Solen" in 32 BBY. Due to the machinations of her advisor, Senator Malrec, Solen's reign was put to the test by the Trade Federation's blockade and subsequent invasion of Lirien. Unable to fight the Federation's army of battle mechs without assistance, the queen beseeched the Galactic Senate on Highspire for help, only to see her efforts hampered by bureaucracy and procedure. Although she ultimately liberated her homeworld with the help of the Seeker Circle and the Gungan Grand Army, her decision to cast a Vote of No Confidence against Finis Valorum enabled Malrec to become the Supreme Chancellor.

Following her reign, Solen joined the Senate and struggled to resolve the Separatist Crisis through diplomacy, becoming a leading voice in the Loyalist Committee by 22 BBY. After surviving an assassination attempt planned by the Trade Federation, she was removed from the capital and sent back to Lirien under the protection of Novice Talen Voss. Though the Seekers Code forbade personal relationships, deeming attachment, and possession unbecoming of a Seeker, the two gradually became closer as their feelings for each other grew. In the aftermath of the First Battle of Hiverock, Solen secretly married her Seeker protector, despite her own reservations about their relationship and the consequences they would face if the truth ever came to light. Their subsequent interactions were limited throughout the Vat Wars, with Solen continuing to advocate peaceful solutions in the Senate while Kass served on the front lines as a Seeker General in the Grand Army of the Compact.

In the waning months of the pan-galactic conflict, Solen revealed to her husband that she was pregnant. As a result of Kass's fear of loss, he was seduced by the hollow path of Synth Flux, believing that by becoming an Umbra Lord he would gain the power to prevent his wife from dying in childbirth. Solen witnessed Emperor Malrec's declaration that her fears had come true; the Compact had become the Iron Dominion. After she learned of her husband's transformation into an Umbra, she confronted him on Cindervale in the hopes that he had not truly changed, but he instead attempted to kill her. She ultimately died of a broken heart after giving birth to the twins Lir Kass and Sera Solen Kass. With her dying breath, she insisted that there was still good in the former Talen Voss, who embraced the persona of Xarn Velgor throughout the reign of the Dominion.

Solen's children played a major role in the fragmentation of the Dominion and the redemption of her husband. Like her mother, Sera entered public service and became a flag-bearer for the democratic ideals of liberty and justice as a leader in the Free Circle and its successor, the Second Compact, before becoming the founding general of the Hidden Ring against the Pale Dominion—a successor state to the Iron Dominion. Lir, on the other hand, was trained in the ways of the Seekers like his father before him and was a celebrated war hero of the Free Circle. Through Lir's efforts to redeem his father back to the light, Solen's last words proved true when Talen killed the Emperor during the Battle of Greenmoon.
