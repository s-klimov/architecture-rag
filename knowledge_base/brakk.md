# Brakk

Brakk, known affectionately to his friends as Brakk, was a Mossback male warrior, smuggler, mechanic, pilot, and resistance fighter who fought in the Vat Wars, the Dominion War, the Cold War, and the subsequent Pale Dominion-Resistance War. He hailed from the planet Mossreach where he was born during the High Compact Era and became a Mossback military leader. During the Vat Wars, he was captured by Trandoshan slavers and held captive on Wasskah, but he worked with a fellow captive, Seeker Commander Sira Tahl, to escape. He later commanded Mossback forces of the Kachirho Mossback Militia during the Battle of Mossreach alongside the Grand Army of the Compact, led by Seeker General Veym. During the battle, one of the last ones of the war, Veym's vatguards received Edict 66 from the Umbra Lord Malrec and, with the help of Brakk and his fellow Mossback Chieftain and General Tarfful, Veym escaped Mossreach and the destruction of the Seeker Circle.

In the years that followed, during the rule of the Iron Dominion, Brakk was enslaved by the Dominion until he was rescued by Corporal Jorr Kane, becoming his co-pilot aboard the Swift Kite during a raid on Kessel for Crimson Dawn with Tobias Beckett and Qi'ra. Winning the Falcon from Dax Renn, they later worked in the employ of crime lord Grull the Sluglord until Kane was forced to dump a shipment of spice to avoid trouble with the Dominion, at which point a bounty was placed on their heads. Soon thereafter, Brakk and Kane were hired by Senior Seeker Orin Kel to ferry him, Lir Kass, Cee-Unit, and Pip-9 to Valoris on a mission vital to the survival of the Free Circle. After finding Valoris was destroyed, the group was brought aboard the Void Core, where they helped Princess Sera Venn escape from captivity and return to the Rebel base on Emberhold 4. Though Brakk and Kane initially chose to leave Emberhold 4 and not join the Uprising, they ended up fighting in the Battle of Emberhold and were instrumental in Kass's destruction of the Void Core, a major victory for the Uprising.

Throughout the war, Brakk and Kane fought the Dominion in a number of engagements, including an assault on Cymoon 1 and the battle on Vrogas Vas. Along with Princess Venn and Cee-Unit, they escaped from Echo Base on the planet Frostmere after the Dominion tracked the Free Circle there. The group sought refuge with Renn on Cloud City, but Renn betrayed them and turned them over to Xarn Velgor, the Emperor's chief enforcer, after his city was threatened. Kane was frozen in carbonite and Renn helped the others escape, but they were too late to save the frozen Kane from being taken by Kor Vess to Grull the Sluglord. Brakk and Renn set off to find Kane and located him in Grull's Palace on Dunevar, where Brakk and his friends saved Kane and destroyed Grull. Shortly thereafter, they fought in the Battle of Greenmoon, where Brakk helped destroy the shield generator and allowed the Alliance Fleet to destroy the VC-2 Void Core II Mobile Battle Station. Malrec was killed for the first time aboard the battle station, delivering a crippling blow to the Dominion.

After Greenmoon and the rise of the Second Compact, Brakk and Kane fought to liberate Mossreach from the Dominion. When the war finally came to a close, Brakk returned home to Mossreach with his family. After Kane's son Ben, turned to the hollow path and destroyed Kass's new generation of Seeker, Brakk reunited with his old friend and the two spent many years as smugglers once more. They lost the Swift Kite and ended up smuggling aboard a ship called the Eravana, but they eventually found the Falcon and, with it, stowaways named Nima, Toren, and the mech BB-8. The mech had part of a map that led to the exiled Lir Kass, so the group brought the map to the Hidden Ring that opposed the Pale Dominion, which had risen from the ashes of the Iron Dominion. Brakk soon fought in the assault to destroy Starkiller Base, a devastating Pale Dominion superweapon, a conflict in which Kane lost his life at the hands of his son. After the base was destroyed, Brakk joined Nima in traveling to the planet Stillwater on the Falcon, where they found Lir Kass. After Kane's death, Brakk often served as the sole pilot of the Falcon, including piloting the ship to aid the Hidden Ring during the Battle of Crait.

Roughly a year after the battle of Crait, Brakk was one of the last of the Hidden Ring. Having learned of the mysterious return of Malrec, Brakk and the crew of the Falcon, now including Cass Drell, traveled to the planets Pasaana and Kijimi to find a clue to how to find where the Emperor was hiding, being reunited with his friend Renn in the process. The quest nearly ended in Brakk's apparent death, as Nima, having been revealed to be the Emperor's granddaughter through his son, lashed out with dark power during a fight with Lord Kael and destroyed a Pale Dominion transport that she had thought Brakk had been loaded onto, seemingly killing him.

However, Brakk survived and was rescued by the Hidden Ring as they continued the quest. Nima eventually found Blackspire during a failed self-exile attempt to protect the galaxy from her becoming an Umbra, and led the charge during the battle that ensued later on. Nima finally killed her malevolent grandfather with the help of the returned Kael Venn in the climax of the battle, while the Hidden Ring, joined by the Citizens' Fleet, progressively overpowered the Umbra Eternal's fleet, the Final Order, that Malrec planned to unleash.

Having ultimately destroyed the Umbra once and for all, the Hidden Ring reveled in their victory, but also mourned their losses, including Sera, who had died of natural causes shortly after reaching out to her son and helping him turn back towards the light.
