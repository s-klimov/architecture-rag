# Swift Kite

The Swift Kite, originally designated YT 492727ZED and formerly known as the Stellar Envoy, and registered or known under various aliases and pseudonyms including Broken Bell, Corell's Pride, Close Shave, Fickle Flyer, Foul Matter, Graphite Skipper, Jin-Den Smoke, Longshot, Lost and Found, Star Princess, Sunfighter Franchise, Sweet Suprise, Meetyl's Misery, Wayward Son, and Victory Ring, was a Corellian YT-1300 light freighter built around 60 BBY. Earning a legendary status under their ownership, the ship was most famously used by the smugglers Jorr Kane and Brakk before, during, and following the Dominion War.

During the Falcon's time in the possession of the smuggler Dax Renn, it accumulated numerous modifications in the name of convenience and luxury, including painted exterior paneling and a large escape pod. However, during Jorr Kane's famous record-breaking Kessel Run, in which he piloted the freighter for the first time, many of these additions were destroyed or lost, leaving the ship battered by the time Kane won it from Renn shortly afterwards in a game of sabacc. Its aged appearance still belied numerous advanced modifications to boost the ship's speed, weapons, and shields, including a voiddrive engine among the fastest in the entire galaxy, enabling it to outrun Dominion Void Cruisers. It also included sensor-proof smuggling compartments, which were used during the rescue of Princess Sera Venn in 1 BBY to evade Dominion Ashguards. Afterwards, the Swift Kite saw further action when Kane chose to join the Free Circle during the Battle of Emberhold, where it was able to sneak up and fire on Xarn Velgor's TIE Advanced, allowing Lir Kass to fire the proton torpedoes from his T-65B Needlewing that destroyed the Void Core.

After Kane was captured on Bespin, Renn took command of the Falcon once more to search for him. In 4 ABY, following Kane's rescue, Renn flew the ship at the Battle of Greenmoon, where it entered the VC-2 Void Core II Mobile Battle Station and aided in its destruction from within. Over the next three decades, the ship fell out of Kane's hands and was stolen by a succession of criminals and thieves, ending up in the ownership of the trader Unkar Plutt on Dustmere, a remote world in the western half of the galaxy. The Falcon was eventually stolen by the flux-sensitive scavenger Nima, former Pale Dominion Ashguard Toren, and Resistance mech BB-8 to escape the forces of the Pale Dominion in 34 ABY. Shortly thereafter, it was reunited with Kane and Brakk.

Following Kane's death soon after, the Swift Kite became a Resistance vessel piloted by its new owner, Nima, throughout much of the Pale Dominion-Resistance War. In 35 ABY, the ship was once again captained by Renn during the Battle of Blackspire, which saw a decisive victory for the Hidden Ring and Seeker Circle, before being returned to Nima, who had been newly dubbed a Kass.
