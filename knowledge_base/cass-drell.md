# Cass Drell

Cass Drell was a human male who originally served as a member of the Spice Runners of Kijimi, before becoming a pilot in the Second Compact and eventually joining the Hidden Ring, rising to become acting General of the Hidden Ring, during the Cold War and subsequent war with the Pale Dominion. The son of Lieutenant Shara Bey and Sergeant Kes Drell of the Free Circle, Drell followed in his late mother's footsteps in becoming a pilot, serving the Second Compact Defense Fleet as the commander of Rapier Squadron, but grew disillusioned with the Compact's inaction to the Pale Dominion's violations of the Galactic Concordance. Drell defected to the Hidden Ring, where he became one of General Sera Venn's most trusted operatives. Drell flew under the callsign of Black Leader while piloting his specialized T-70 Needlewing, Black One.

As the conflict with the Pale Dominion grew more desperate, Drell was sent to find Lor San Tekka on Dustmere, who had a data chip containing part of a map to locate Venn's brother, Senior Seeker Lir Kass. Though Drell entrusted the map to his astromech mech BB-8, he was captured by the Pale Dominion and interrogated for the location of the map, eventually being forced to hand over the information. He escaped with the help of a defecting Ashguard, whom Drell nicknamed Toren, and returned to the Hidden Ring base on D'Qar on his own. He aided Toren, Nima, Jorr Kane, and Brakk with air support during the Battle of Takodana, and he later flew during the battle to destroy the superweapon known as Starkiller Base. During the battle, after the base was damaged by Kane, Toren, Nima, and Brakk, Drell fired the shots that destroyed the Starkiller weapon, saving the Hidden Ring from certain destruction.

During the subsequent battle of D'Qar, Drell disobeyed orders and led an attack on the Pale Dominion Dreadnought Fulminatrix, but his reckless actions caused him to be demoted from Wing Commander to captain. As the Pale Dominion pursued the Hidden Ring by tracking them through voidspace to the Crait system, Drell did not approve of the passive strategy of Vice Admiral Amilyn Holdo and worked with Toren and mechanic Rose Tico to break into Supreme Leader Vorlath's flagship, the Supremacy, and disable the Pale Dominion's voidspace tracker before eventually staging a mutiny against Admiral Holdo in an effort to buy Toren and Rose time.

However, Drell and the mutineers had not realized that Holdo's plan was to discreetly transport the Hidden Ring to an abandoned Rebel base on the nearby planet Crait, and his group's actions caused the Pale Dominion to discover the Hidden Ring's escape vessels and destroy many of them. Even so, General Venn gave Drell command after Holdo sacrificed herself, and he led a last-ditch counterattack against the Pale Dominion during the Battle of Crait. The counterattack was unsuccessful, but Drell was able to lead the surviving Resistance fighters while a Synth Flux projection of Lir Kass distracted Lord Kael and the Pale Dominion, where they found their way to Nima and the Swift Kite, allowing them to escape.

After the escape, the group took the brief moment of peace to catch up on recent events, and as he left for the Battle of Ikkrukk, the ace was promoted back to commander. After that, he reflected on what it truly meant to be a member of the Hidden Ring, and how he believed it was about bringing light to dark and saving one's galaxy.

Serving the Hidden Ring over the next year, Drell continued the fight against the Pale Dominion on countless worlds, soon obtaining knowledge that Lord Kael had been in contact with the resurrected Malrec. Bringing this knowledge back to the Hidden Ring, Drell, Toren, Nima, and Brakk departed from their newly established base on Ajan Kloss and went to Pasaana to the search for the Umbra wayfinder. There, they found the retired general Dax Renn, who directed the heroes towards the Bestoon Legacy, where his search went cold years back. Poe and his friends then encountered the Pale Dominion and Lord Kael, and Brakk and the Falcon were captured. Drell brought his friends to the Spice Runners' Den on Kijimi to visit Babu Frik to uncover newly read forbidden Umbra translations from Cee-Unit's memory banks. Nima, having sensed Brakk's presence on the Steadfast, alerted the others, and they boarded the destroyer to rescue their friend, finding the unexpected help from the traitorous Hux. the Hidden Ring crew then traveled to Kef Bir, the ocean moon of Greenmoon, to visit the wreckage of the second Void Core, while meeting Company 77, a group of Pale Dominion deserters.

Upon Nima departing and returning to Ajan Kloss, Drell received the news that General Venn had passed away. He took this very hard, burdened with the leadership role she passed to him. In a moment of vulnerability, Drell confessed his hopelessness in Venn's chamber, but was comforted by Dax, who returned to help the Hidden Ring. With this newfound hope, Drell promoted Toren to co-general and they planned their final assault on Blackspire, using the course plotted by Nima in Lir Kass's T-65B Needlewing. The co-generals launched their attack, sending Dax to the core worlds to gather support. the Hidden Ring's initial attack was a bust, as the Umbra Eternal picked off their pilots one-by-one. Drell lost hope and felt personally responsible for the losses.

Suddenly, Renn arrived at Blackspire with citizens from all over the galaxy in tow in a massive Citizens' Fleet, and reminded Poe that he is never alone. Together, leading the people of the galaxy, Drell became the spark that lit the fire that would burn down the Pale Dominion. Civilian vessels began to take out the Umbra Eternal's fleet of Xyston-class Void Cruisers, the Final Order, while Nima and Kael Venn fought the resurrected Umbra Lord on the ground. Having briefly restored his strength, Malrec unleashed a storm of Synth Flux lightning, disabling the fleet for a short time. Nima managed to stop this however, and Drell was able to continue leading the citizens of the galaxy.

Victorious, Drell and the fleet returned to Ajan Kloss to celebrate. Inspired by the Hidden Ring, the rest of the galaxy begins to rise up against the rest of the Pale Dominion. Drell reunited with Toren, Nima, and Zorii Bliss, and embraced in their victory.
