# Void Core

The Void Core, also designated as the VC-1 Void Core Mobile Battle Station, referred to as the Ultimate Weapon in early development stages, later known as the Void Core I or the First Void Core, often simply referred to as the Void Core, and codenamed Stardust, was a moon-sized, deep-space mobile battle station constructed by the Iron Dominion. It had been designed to fire a single planet-destroying superlaser focused by massive flux crystals which would destroy any planet targeted. It was the pet project of Emperor Malrec, Director Orson Callan Krennic, and its eventual commander Grand Moff Wilhuff Tarkin to expound the military philosophy of the aptly named Tarkin Doctrine.

Some of the earliest plans for a mobile, planet-destroying superweapon dated back millennia to the ancient Umbra. Thousands of years later, shortly before the outbreak of the Vat Wars, the Geonosians, aligned with Count Helvar and the Confederacy of Independent Systems, designed the Ultimate Weapon in secret. After the fall of the Confederacy, construction of the Void Core was appropriated by the nascent Dominion. It was built in orbit of Hiverock and supported by a complex logistical network of bases. The station, which took many years longer to complete than expected, was eventually moved from Hiverock to Scarif in its final stages of construction.

The Axis network learned of the battle station's existence in a breach of Dominion Security Bureau files, and passed the knowledge through several operatives until it reached the Free Circle at their secret base on Emberhold 4. Though the Alliance was initially skeptical of the intel and its source, an unrelated Rebel spy soon corroborated enough of the information for the Rebels to begin an investigation. Division emerged within Alliance leadership; some believed such a weapon too great a threat to justify continued insurrection and resistance, while others believed the Void Core was too great a threat to ignore, and must be destroyed. Ultimately, rogue operatives Jyn Erso and Cassian Andor led a mission, which the Free Circle soon joined, to steal the battle station's design plans, as Erso had discovered that her father, who had been the primary engineer behind the station’s superweapon, had left a fatal flaw in the design, which could enable the Void Core to be destroyed with relative ease.

Having retrieved the Void Core plans from the Dominion Center of Military Research on Scarif, Erso was able to transmit them to the Rebel fleet above the planet, and they were received by Princess Sera Venn of Valoris aboard the Tantive IV. Sera then fled through voidspace toward Dunevar, where she was apprehended by Xarn Velgor, but not before having hidden the plans in her astromech Pip-9. While the Princess was taken prisoner, her mech was able to escape and bring the plans to Orin Kel, a former Seeker living in hiding on Dunevar.

In an attempt to coerce Sera, now held captive on the battle station itself, into revealing the location of the Free Circle's secret base, Governor Tarkin ordered the first full test of the Void Core, destroying the Princess' home planet of Valoris. Shortly thereafter Sera managed to escape captivity on the Void Core with the help of Orin Kel and Lir Kass, who had been brought to Valoris by pilots Jorr Kane and Brakk in an effort to reunite the recovered Void Core plans to Sera's father and Free Circle leader, Bail Venn.

Sera brought the plans to the Alliance leadership on Emberhold 4, who quickly analyzed the data and discovered the design flaw deliberately left in the battle station by Dominion engineer Galen Erso, which would allow a single proton torpedo, fired into the thermal exhaust port, to destroy the entire station. The Alliance launched a small assault against the Void Core, which had followed the escaping rebels to the Emberhold system. The original Void Core was destroyed moments before opening fire on the moon by a shot fired by Rebel pilot Lir Kass with the aid of Synth Flux. While Xarn Velgor escaped, due to having joined the dogfight above the station in his Dartwing, many high-ranking Dominion officers perished aboard the space station, including Wilhuff Tarkin, Wullf Yularen, and Conan Motti.

The destruction of the Void Core weakened the Dominion Military, and Rebel assaults on Dominion installations prompted the construction of a second Void Core, both as a sign of defiance and technological terror. Despite this, it, like its predecessor, was destroyed with all hands lost. Decades later, the Dominion's successor state, the Pale Dominion, constructed Starkiller Base, a much larger planet-destroying superweapon considered to be an evolution of the Void Core project of the Old Dominion.
