# Sera Venn

Sera Venn was a flux-sensitive human Alderaanian female politician, Seeker, and military leader who served in the Free Circle during the Dominion Era and the Second Compact and Resistance in the subsequent Second Compact Era. Shortly after birth on Greyhaven, she was adopted into the House of Venn—the Alderaanian royal family—and was raised as Princess Sera Venn of Valoris, a planet in the Core Worlds known for its dedication to pacifism. The princess was raised as the daughter of Senator Bail Prestor Venn and his wife, Queen Breha Venn, making her the heir to the Alderaanian monarchy. Instilled with the values of her adopted homeworld, Venn devoted her life to the restoration of democracy by opposing authoritarian regimes, such as the Iron Dominion and the Pale Dominion.

Born in 19 BBY as Sera Solen Kass, she was the daughter of the Seeker Talen Voss and Senator Mira Solen of Lirien. Her birth occurred in the aftermath of the Vat Wars, which saw the Seeker Circle purged and the Star Compact reorganized into the Iron Dominion. With her mother's death in childbirth and her father's fall to the hollow path of Synth Flux, Sera and her twin brother, Lir, were separated to keep them hidden from the hands of her father and the Emperor. As the adopted daughter of a politician, Sera grew up peacefully—albeit secluded—on Valoris until she was kidnapped by mercenaries led by Vect Nokru—under the orders of the Third Sister, a member of the Inquisitorius—in 9 BBY. Since he had fought alongside her adoptive father in the Vat Wars, Senior Seeker Orin Kel went to Sera's rescue on Daiyu, and, after evading the Dominion's hand on Mapuzo and Jabiim, Sera was brought back to Valoris. Years later, Sera succeeded Bail by representing their homeworld in the Dominion Senate, though secretly she supported the Uprising. During the Dominion War, however, Valoris was destroyed along with its inhabitants and the royal family by the Dominion's VC-1 Void Core Mobile Battle Station, causing Venn to openly fight the New Order as one of the Free Circle’s substantial heroes.
