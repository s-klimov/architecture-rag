# Jorr Kane

Jorr Kane, known only as Jorr until being given the surname Kane, was a human male smuggler and hero of the Free Circle. He rose from an impoverished childhood to become captain of the Swift Kite, a leader in the Free Circle and an instrumental figure in the Dominion War during the reign of the Iron Dominion. Although he did not believe in Synth Flux controlling his destiny, Jorr came to believe in the cause of galactic freedom, joining Seeker Lir Kass and Princess Sera Venn in the fight for the Uprising. Following the war, Jorr and Sera welcomed a son, Kael Venn, who became the dark knight Lord Kael after being corrupted by the hollow path of Synth Flux. Although their relationship floundered, Sera hoped that Jorr, as Ben's father, could find a way to break the hollow path's hold on their son. Jorr Kane became legendary across the galaxy as both a smuggler and war hero of the Uprising.

As a young man fighting for survival on the mean streets of Corellia, Jorr yearned to fly among the stars with dreams of becoming the galaxy's best pilot. With a fellow scrumrat named Qi'ra, Jorr lived as a street thief with Corellia's notorious White Worms gang until an important deal with Lady Proxima went bad, causing Jorr to flee Corellia. He was given the full name "Jorr Kane" by an Dominion recruitment officer while entering the Dominion Academy. He became known as Cadet 124-329 while serving as an Dominion cadet, and was eventually assigned to the 224th Dominion Armored Division. On Mimban, Jorr met the thief Tobias Beckett and wound up deserting the Dominion and joining his crew to steal unrefined coaxium from Kessel in order to pay a debt to Dryden Vos, a kingpin with the Crimson Dawn crime syndicate. By the end of this scheme, during which he reunited with Qi'ra, Jorr was forced to kill his mentor Beckett after a brutal double-cross. Jorr also became rivals and close friends with fellow smuggler Dax Renn and the Mossback Brakk, the latter who became his best friend. Renn owned the Swift Kite, a fast ship with which Jorr made the Kessel Run in just twelve parsecs and won from Dax in a game of sabacc.

With only Brakk remaining at his side, as his trusted copilot who swore a life debt to the Corellian smuggler, Jorr Kane embarked on a smuggling career under the employ of Grull the Sluglord, a notorious gangster and crime lord of Dunevar. After ditching a shipment of spice to avoid trouble with the Dominion, Kane owed a great deal of money to Grull, who then placed a bounty on the smuggler's head, as a result. His fortune seemed to have changed when Jorr and Brakk were hired to charter Lir Kass, Orin Kel, and the mechs Pip-9 and Cee-Unit to Valoris, avoiding Dominion Ashguards who were searching for Lir's mechs. However, as they find Valoris destroyed by the Dominion's new superweapon, the Void Core, Kane became caught up in the Uprising against the Dominion, rescuing Princess Sera Venn, delivering the stolen Void Core plans to the hidden Rebel base on Emberhold 4, and saved Lir from the Umbra Lord Xarn Velgor in the Battle of Emberhold, allowing Kass to destroy the Void Core. Kane fought with the Uprising for a number of years afterward, taking part in numerous operations and battles against the Dominion.

After the Battle of Frostmere, Kane sought refuge with Venn—with whom he had fallen in love—Brakk, and Cee-Unit on Cloud City, which was operated by his old friend Dax Renn. There, they were captured by Xarn Velgor as part of a trap to bait Kass to the city. Kane was frozen in carbonite and taken to Grull's Palace on Dunevar by the bounty hunter Kor Vess, where he remained encased in carbonite for months until he was rescued by his friends. After returning to the Alliance Fleet, Kane was promoted to the rank of general and was given command of the Pathfinders, a special forces squad, for the Battle of Greenmoon. Kane led the Pathfinders to Greenmoon, where he, Venn, Brakk, and the special forces team, with the help of the native Smallfolk, destroyed the shield generator protecting the second Void Core that was being constructed in orbit. Their actions allowed the Alliance Fleet to destroy the superweapon.

The Battle of Greenmoon, which saw the deaths of Emperor Malrec and Xarn Velgor, allowed the Uprising to form the Second Compact. In the early days after Greenmoon, Kane continued to lead the Pathfinders against the Dominion. He married Venn, and the two had a son, Kael Venn. The boy struggled with the hollow path of Synth Flux—like his mother, who was the daughter of Xarn Velgor and twin sister of Lir Kass, Ben was flux-sensitive—so Venn sent him to be trained as a Seeker by Kass. Ben was seduced to the hollow path by Vorlath, Supreme Leader of the Pale Dominion, resulting in the destruction of Kass's attempt to rebuild the Seeker Circle.

Having lost their son, Kane left and returned to smuggling, and even lost the Swift Kite to numerous thieves. He continued to smuggle until he was brought back into the fight by a scavenger named Nima and a defected Pale Dominion Ashguard named Toren, who carried with them a mech, BB-8, that had part of a map to Kass's location. Kane, who retrieved the Falcon from Nima and Toren, returned to Venn, who led the Hidden Ring against the Pale Dominion, and fought in the battle to destroy the Pale Dominion superweapon known as Starkiller Base. During the battle, Kane saw his son, who had taken the name Lord Kael, and tried to convince him to return home. Instead, Ren stabbed his father with his fluxblade. Mortally wounded, Kane fell to his death in the bowels of the Starkiller weapon. However, Kane's heroic memory survived and his spirit remained alive and appeared in a Synth Flux vision a year later to convince Ren to abandon the hollow path and defeat the resurrected Malrec, after a duel on the remains of the second Void Core between Nima and Ren, being enough for Kael Venn to return and eventually sacrifice his own life to help Nima end Malrec and the Umbra for good.
