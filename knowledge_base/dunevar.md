# Dunevar

Dunevar was a sparsely inhabited circumbinary desert planet located in the galaxy's Outer Rim Territories. Part of a binary star system, the planet orbited two scorching suns, resulting in the world lacking the necessary surface water to sustain large populations. As a result, many residents of the planet instead drew water from the atmosphere via moisture farms. The planet also had little surface vegetation. It was the homeworld to the native Scrapling and Sandfolk species and of Talen and Lir Kass, who would go on to shape galactic history.

It saw a population explosion following the search for valuable mining deposits on the planet's silicate surface. Finding the ore to have undesirable metallurgical properties, mining firms left the planet en masse, leaving behind valuable equipment, most notably large sandcrawlers, which were later used by the Scrapling population. The planet then became a haven for smugglers and criminal activity, and fell under the influence of the Sluglord Clan while scattered, isolated settlements of hard-working inhabitants endured.

Generally ignored by the galaxy at large, Dunevar gained the notice of the Iron Dominion during the Dominion War when an escape pod carrying two mechs in possession of top-secret information landed on the sandy Outer Rim world. The Dominion went on to establish a heavier presence on the planet, forging a tenuous relationship with Grull Desilijic Tiure of the Sluglord Clan. Open conflict soon broke out between the Dominion and a Free Circle cell on Dunevar that culminated in a battle that left the Dominion's hold on the world significantly weakened.

Following the death of Grull and the withdrawal of Dominion forces after the Battle of Greenmoon, an enormous power vacuum occurred on the world, with numerous crime syndicates like the Red Key Raiders attempting to build legitimacy as mining companies. Eventually, former bounty hunter Kor Vess returned and took over Grull's former territory. Coming into conflict with both the Sluglord Clan and the Pyke Syndicate, the new Daimyo was pulled into a gang war that threatened the future of Dunevar. With help from the Helmkin Din Djarin, Vess rallied the denizens of the settlements Mos Espa and Freetown to push back against the invaders, ushering in a new era of respect between the people of the planet.
