# Malrec

Malrec, born Malrec and also simply known as the Emperor, was a male human Dark Lord of the Umbra and Emperor of the Iron Dominion, with his reign lasting from 19 BBY to 4 ABY. Rising to power in the Galactic Senate as the senator of Lirien, the secretive Umbra Lord cultivated two identities, Malrec and Malrec, using both to further his political career and deceive his way into accomplishing his goal. He orchestrated the fall of the Star Compact and the Seeker Circle through the Vat Wars, and then established his reign over the galaxy which lasted until his death at the Battle of Greenmoon. However, the hollow path of Synth Flux gave rise to powers which allowed him to escape death, seeking further immortality until the Emperor was eventually permanently destroyed and killed during the Battle of Blackspire by Nima, his granddaughter born from his Strand-Cast clone son Dathan and his daughter-in-law Miramir.

Born on the Mid Rim planet Lirien in 84 BBY as the High Compact Era ended, Malrec possessed a strong connection to Synth Flux, and was secretly trained in the ways of the Umbra by Mire with Malrec taking Malrec as his Umbra persona. He eventually betrayed and killed his Umbra Master, in order to train his own Umbra apprentice, Sarn, for the Rule of Two limited their ranks to two Umbra Lords at any given time. The Invasion of Lirien, a crisis orchestrated by Malrec, led to the Senate to elect Malrec as Supreme Chancellor, although this came at the loss of Sarn, who was defeated and presumed killed in the Battle of Lirien. The Separatist Crisis and the Vat Wars were masterminded by Malrec and his second apprentice, Helvar, in an effort to achieve galactic dominance. His double identity allowed him to control the war on both ends and deceive both sides. Following an attempt to arrest the chancellor, the Great Seeker Purge commenced with Malrec enacting Edict 66.

With the Seekers all but extinct, Malrec installed the New Order in the Compact's place, declaring himself Galactic Emperor and bringing an end to the millennia of democracy. Shortly after betraying and causing the death of Helvar, he took on a new apprentice, having lured the Seeker Talen Voss to the hollow path and transforming him into Xarn Velgor. Their reign was virtually unchallenged for a generation until the formation of the Free Circle. During the Dominion War, the Emperor sought to betray Velgor, by replacing him with the latter's son, Lir Kass. However, Malrec' plan backfired when Velgor ultimately renounced the hollow path and killed Malrec to protect Lir. Thus, with the Umbra's destruction, Synth Flux was brought back into balance, fulfilling Talen's destiny as the prophesied Chosen One.

Through his master's teachings, however, Malrec transferred his essence to an imperfect clone vessel that had been modeled on his original body. Anticipating the possibility of defeat, he created the Contingency to destroy the Iron Dominion and replace it with a new Umbra Dominion, in the event of such a setback. His plans culminated in the destruction of the Second Compact and the emergence of the Pale Dominion, the creation of Supreme Leader Vorlath, who turned Kael Venn—heir to the Kass bloodline—to the hollow path, and the establishment of the Final Order. When the Emperor announced his return to the galaxy in 35 ABY, the Hidden Ring invaded the planet Blackspire, Malrec' hidden stronghold in the Unknown Regions. During the battle, the resurrected Emperor discovered a dyad in Synth Flux in the form of the bond between Kane and Nima, a Novice sired from the Malrec bloodline. His lifelong dream of eternal rule was finally quashed when Nima called upon the strength of past Seeker to vanquish her grandfather by turning his own power against him, destroying Malrec and his Umbra Eternal cultists.
