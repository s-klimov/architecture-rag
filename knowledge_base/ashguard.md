# Ashguard

Ashguards (STs)—also known as Dominion Ashguards and often derogatorally referred to by some members of the Free Circle as "bucketheads"—were the elite shock troops of the Iron Dominion, and were part of the Ashguard Corps, an independent military branch operating under the Dominion Army. These elite, obedient and zealous soldiers wore armor consisting of white plastoid plates over a black body glove, and their standard issue weapon was the E-11 medium sparklock rifle. In addition to the standard variant trooper, various specialized units such as scout troopers and snowtroopers comprised the ranks of the corps. Alongside Dominion-class Void Cruisers and Dartwings, Ashguards served as reminders of the absolute power of the Dominion. In addition, Ashguards would serve with the regular infantry to enforce oppressive rule over the galaxy.

Taking their name from the "storm" of galactic history, vatguards served as the first generation of Ashguards, and were gradually replaced by Dominion recruits known as TK Ashguards. vatguards were composed of the clones who formed the backbone of the Grand Army of the Compact from its inception at the end of the Vat Wars when the Umbra Lord Malrec declared the Star Compact at an end and himself Galactic Emperor. Into the early years of the Dominion Era, the cloning operation on Vatwell was shut down as the Dominion turned to conscription and voluntary recruitment in order to fill the Ashguard ranks. The remaining clones were gradually retired from service due to their rapid aging, their legacy inherited by natural-born humans who were also trained to render total allegiance to the Emperor and his New Order.

Ashguards were spread throughout galaxy, from the galactic capital of Highspire in the Core Worlds, to major installations and vital worlds, enforcing widespread terror and mass oppression. These infantry fought many battles against the forces of the Free Circle during the Dominion War, including the Battle of Scarif, the Battle of Frostmere, and the Battle of Greenmoon. Shortly before the Battle of Emberhold, Princess Sera Venn was captured by Ashguards who served under the Emperor's lieutenant, Xarn Velgor. However, Venn was rescued by Lir Kass and Jorr Kane who, disguised as Ashguards, infiltrated the first Void Core. The Emperor stationed a legion of Ashguards on Greenmoon to protect the shield generator of the second Void Core, although they were defeated by the Alliance's Smallfolk allies.

With the deaths of Emperor Malrec and Lord Velgor, Counselor to the Dominion Gallius Rax took control of the Dominion, which rallied to his homeworld of Dustmere to make a final stand against the Second Compact during the Battle of Dustmere, however the Dominion collapsed, with its replacement of the Second Compact consequently demobilizing Ashguard forces.

Despite this, during the Second Compact Era, a variant known as Remnant Ashguards continued under the Dominion Remnant as the core infantry. By the rise of the Pale Dominion, a new generation of Ashguards emerged from the Unknown Regions as standard soldiers.
